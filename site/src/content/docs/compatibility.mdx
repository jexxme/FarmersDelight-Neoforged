Farmer's Delight Neoforged runs on NeoForge and needs no other mods. This page is for picking a modpack: which versions it runs on and what it does with the mods around it.

## Minecraft versions

There's one file per Minecraft version, all with the same content ({versionLabel()}). Install it on the client and the server.

## Recipe viewers and other helpers

| Mod | Works? |
|---|---|
| **JEI** | Yes, on every version |
| **REI** | Yes, on Minecraft 26.1.2 and newer |
| **EMI** | Not yet: EMI isn't out for Minecraft 26.x |
| **AppleSkin** | Yes |
| **CraftTweaker** | Not yet: CraftTweaker isn't out for Minecraft 26.x |

**JEI and REI** both show the Cooking Pot, Cutting Board and Organic Compost recipes, and list the Stove and Skillet for campfire cooking. Click the arrow in the Cooking Pot to see its recipes, or use the viewer's transfer button to move a recipe's ingredients into the pot. Info pages explain wheat dough, straw, ham, knives and the wild crops; every one of the {knives.length} knives has its info page in both.

**AppleSkin** reads food values from the game, so it shows hunger and saturation for every Farmer's Delight food without any special support. While you have Nourishment, Farmer's Delight gilds your hunger bar itself; you can turn that off in the [client settings](/reference/config/#enableNourishmentHungerOverlay).

**CraftTweaker** support will come back once CraftTweaker is out for 26.x. Until then, datapacks can change all of Farmer's Delight's recipes.

## Tags for other mods

## Works with Vine & Barrel

[Vine & Barrel](https://jexxme.github.io/VineAndBarrel/) is a cozy brewing mod: beer, wine, mead and cider. It's a
separate mod by the same author and works fine on its own. Install both and these extras switch on by themselves:

- The <Item id="stove" /> and Farmer's Delight's other heat sources heat Vine & Barrel's Brew Kettle.
- Grape and hop bushes climb <Item id="rope" label="ropes" /> like tomatoes do.
- <Item id="rich_soil" /> and <Item id="rich_soil_farmland" /> speed up grapes, hops and barley.
- <Item id="apple_cider" /> and <Item id="melon_juice" /> age in the Aging Barrel into Hard Cider and Melon Wine.
- The <Item id="cooking_pot" /> cooks with its drinks and barley: Beer-Battered Fish, Wine-Braised Beef, Cider-Braised
  Pork, White Wine Risotto and Barley Mutton Broth. Like Farmer's Delight's own meals, they come in a bowl and give
  [Nourishment](/mechanics/effects/).
- **Coq au Vin** is a feast from the pot: place it and serve four bowls.
- **Beer Bread** is crafted from wheat and a Pale Ale or Wheat Beer.
- A knife gets <Item id="straw" /> from ripe barley, and the <Item id="cutting_board" /> splits Wild Barley into barley and, sometimes, straw.

The full list is on Vine & Barrel's [Farmer's Delight page](https://jexxme.github.io/VineAndBarrel/farmers-delight/).
