Farmer's Delight Neoforged runs on NeoForge and needs no other mods. This page is for picking a modpack: which versions it runs on and what it does with the mods around it.

## Minecraft versions

There's one file per Minecraft version, all with the same content (version {meta.modVersion}). Install it on the client and the server.

## Recipe viewers and other helpers

| Mod | Works? |
|---|---|
| **JEI** | Yes, on every version |
| **REI** | Yes, on Minecraft 26.1.2 and newer |
| **EMI** | Not yet: EMI isn't out for Minecraft 26.x |
| **AppleSkin** | Yes |
| **CraftTweaker** | Not yet: CraftTweaker isn't out for Minecraft 26.x |

**JEI and REI** both show the Cooking Pot, Cutting Board and Organic Compost recipes, and list the Stove and Skillet for campfire cooking. Click the arrow in the Cooking Pot to see its recipes, or use the viewer's transfer button to move a recipe's ingredients into the pot. Info pages explain wheat dough, straw, ham, knives and the wild crops; every one of the {knives.length} knives has its info page in both.

**AppleSkin** reads food values from the game, so it shows hunger and saturation for every Farmer's Delight food without any special support. While you have Nourishment, Farmer's Delight gilds your hunger bar itself; you can turn that off in the [client settings](/reference/config/#enableNourishmentHungerOverlay).

**CraftTweaker** support will come back once CraftTweaker is out for 26.x. Until then, datapacks can change all of Farmer's Delight's recipes.

## Tags for other mods

Farmer's Delight puts its items in the shared tags most mods use, so another mod's recipe that asks for "any onion", "any cabbage" or "any knife" takes Farmer's Delight's too. It also works with a few mods directly:
