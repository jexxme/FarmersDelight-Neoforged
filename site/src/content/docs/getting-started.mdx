Farmer's Delight Neoforged is vectorwing's Farmer's Delight, kept cooking on Minecraft {oldest} to {newest} with NeoForge.

<Aside type="tip" title="Coming from the original Farmer's Delight?">
	Swap the file and carry on. Remove the original first; this mod replaces it. The mod ID is still `farmersdelight`
	and every item and recipe keeps its name, so your worlds, datapacks and add-ons come along. See
	[Differences from the original](/differences/).
</Aside>

Farmer's Delight adds a kitchen to Minecraft. You grow four new crops, cook meals in a pot over a fire, cut ingredients
on a board and set out feasts for your friends. The food is worth it: most meals grant [Nourishment](/mechanics/effects/),
which stops your hunger bar from going down while it lasts.

<Shot src="kitchen.jpg" caption="A Farmer's Delight kitchen: stoves, a cooking pot, cabinets and a cutting board." />

## Install

Farmer's Delight Neoforged needs [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/) and nothing else. Find your Minecraft version and
download from that row. Every row is the same release, {versionLabel()}:

<table>
	<thead>
		<tr><th>Minecraft and NeoForge</th><th>Download</th></tr>
	</thead>
	<tbody>
		{meta.versions.map((v) => (
			<tr>
				<td><strong>{v.gameVersions.replaceAll(',', ', ')}</strong><br />NeoForge {rangeText(v.neoforgeRange)}</td>
				<td>{links(v).map((l, i) => <>{i > 0 && ' · '}<a href={l.href} download={l.download || undefined} style="white-space:nowrap">{l.label}</a></>)}</td>
			</tr>
		))}
	</tbody>
</table>

Put the same file in the `mods` folder of the server and of every player. The <a href={`${REPO}/releases`}>GitHub releases</a>
also have the older versions.

## Where to start

- **New to the mod?** Follow [Your first meal](/guides/first-meal/): from a fresh world to your first bowl of stew, step by step.
- **Want a proper farm?** The [Farming guide](/guides/farming-progression/) takes you from wild crops to Rich Soil and mushroom colonies.
- **Looking something up?** Every item has its own page in [All items & blocks](/items/), and the [Food reference](/reference/food/) compares every meal.
- **Building a modpack?** [Compatibility](/compatibility/) lists what works with JEI, REI, AppleSkin, Create and more, and which recipes and tags other mods can use.
