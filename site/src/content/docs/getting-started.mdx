Farmer's Delight adds a kitchen to Minecraft. You grow four new crops, cook meals in a pot over a fire, cut ingredients
on a board and set out feasts for your friends. The food is worth it: most meals grant <EffectBadge id="farmersdelight:nourishment" name="Nourishment" />,
which stops your hunger bar from going down while it lasts.

<Shot src="kitchen.jpg" caption="A Farmer's Delight kitchen: stoves, a cooking pot, cabinets and a cutting board." />

## Install

Farmer's Delight Neoforged needs [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/) and nothing else. Install it on both the client and the server.
Use the file that matches your Minecraft version:

## Your first hour

<Steps>

1. **Find wild crops.** There are {wildCount} kinds of wild crops in the overworld. Break them for seeds and produce:
   <Item id="wild_cabbages" /> and <Item id="wild_beetroots" /> grow on beaches, <Item id="wild_onions" /> and <Item id="wild_carrots" /> in temperate biomes, <Item id="wild_potatoes" /> in cold ones,
   <Item id="wild_tomatoes" /> in hot biomes and <Item id="wild_rice" /> in shallow water. The [wild crops page](/mechanics/wild-crops/) has the exact biomes.

   <Render name="scenes/wild-crops" size={300} caption="Wild cabbages and beetroots on sand, wild onions, carrots and potatoes on grass." />

2. **Make a knife.** A <Item id="flint_knife" /> is the cheapest. Knives cut ingredients on the Cutting Board, harvest straw from grass
   and get extra drops from animals.

   <RecipeList ids={['flint_knife']} />

3. **Plant a garden.** Plant <Item id="cabbage_seeds" />, <Item id="tomato_seeds" /> and <Item id="onion" /> on farmland. Rice goes into a
   one-block-deep water source. Put rope above your tomatoes and they climb it. Details are on the [crops page](/mechanics/crops/).

4. **Build a kitchen.** Craft a <Item id="cooking_pot" /> and a <Item id="stove" />. The pot only cooks with heat below it. A lit
   stove, a campfire or fire all work; the [heat sources page](/mechanics/heat-sources/) lists them all.

   <RecipeList ids={['cooking_pot', 'stove', 'cutting_board']} />

5. **Cook a meal.** Put the ingredients in the pot over heat and wait. Take the meal out with an empty <Item id="minecraft:bowl" /> in the
   bowl slot. <Item id="vegetable_soup" /> is a good first meal. It restores {stew.food.nutrition} hunger and gives Nourishment.

   <RecipeList ids={['cooking/vegetable_soup']} />

</Steps>
