Farmer's Delight adds a kitchen to Minecraft. You grow four new crops, cook meals in a pot over a fire, cut ingredients
on a board and set out feasts for your friends. The food is worth it: most meals grant <EffectBadge id="farmersdelight:nourishment" name="Nourishment" />,
which stops your hunger bar from going down while it lasts.

<Shot src="kitchen.jpg" caption="A Farmer's Delight kitchen: stoves, a cooking pot, cabinets and a cutting board." />

## Install

Farmer's Delight Neoforged needs [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/) and nothing else. Install it on both the client and the server.
Use the file that matches your Minecraft version:

	<thead>

	</thead>
	<tbody>

Download it from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/farmers-delight-neoforged) or [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/farmers-delight-neoforged).

<Aside type="tip" title="Coming from the original Farmer's Delight?">
	Remove it first; this mod replaces it. Your worlds, datapacks and add-ons keep working, because every item and recipe
	keeps its name. See [Differences from the original](/differences/).
</Aside>

- **Want a proper farm?** The [Farming guide](/guides/farming-progression/) takes you from wild crops to Rich Soil and mushroom colonies.
- **Looking something up?** Every item has its own page in [All items & blocks](/items/), and the [Food reference](/reference/food/) compares every meal.
