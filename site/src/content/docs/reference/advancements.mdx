Farmer's Delight adds its own advancement tab with {meta.counts.advancements} advancements. Each line in the tree leads from an advancement to the ones it unlocks.
The list below the tree gives the description, the frame and what counts: "Any of" means one of the listed criteria is enough, "Needs all" means every one.
