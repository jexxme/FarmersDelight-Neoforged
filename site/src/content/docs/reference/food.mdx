## Reading the table

- **Hunger** is the number of hunger points restored; one shank on the HUD is 2 points.
- **Saturation** is the saturation added. Saturation can never be higher than your current hunger level, so a high value only pays off when you eat it hungry.
- **Sat./pt** is saturation per hunger point. Higher means the food keeps you full longer for the hunger it fills.
- **Effects** show duration as minutes:seconds and a chance when it is below 100%.
  [Nourishment](/mechanics/effects/) stops the hunger bar from draining; it comes in {nourishmentTiers.value.length} lengths: {nourishmentTiers.value.map(([n, t]) => `${n.toLowerCase()} ${duration(t)}`).join(', ')} <Cite c={nourishmentTiers} />.
- **Per stack** is hunger × stack size: how much food one inventory slot holds.
- Meals and drinks give their container back when you finish them, even from a full stack.

Its tooltip still lists Nourishment. The vanilla rows above show what you actually get with the default config.
