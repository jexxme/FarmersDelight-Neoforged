All {itemList.filter((i) => i.food || i.consumable).length} things you can eat or drink, with their real values from the game. The quick answers first, then the full table: sort by any column, pick a kind of food, or tick **Vanilla food too** to compare with Minecraft's own.

<FoodReference />

**Hunger** is what a food fills on your hunger bar; one shank is 2. **Saturation** keeps you full before the bar starts dropping, but you can never have more saturation than hunger, so high-saturation food pays off most when you eat it hungry.
[Nourishment](/mechanics/effects/) stops your hunger bar from draining and comes in {nourishmentTiers.value.length} lengths: {nourishmentTiers.value.map(([, t]) => timeText(t)).join(', ')}. Bowls and bottles come back when you finish a meal or drink, even from a full stack.

Rabbit Stew's tooltip promises {timeText(rabbitSoup.duration)} of Nourishment, but you don't get it. Milk Bottle, Hot Cocoa and Melon Juice do nothing when you drink them; the table marks them. Both are fixed in the next update.
