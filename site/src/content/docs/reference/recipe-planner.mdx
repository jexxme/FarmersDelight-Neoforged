Pick an item, set how many you want, and the planner breaks it down into the recipes behind it, all the way to things you farm, mine or loot.
It starts with a <Item id="roast_chicken_block" />. The tree uses the {meta.counts.recipes} recipes that load in a plain game (no add-on mods).

<RecipePlanner />

## How the planner decides

- **Raw materials.** Vanilla items, crops, wild plants and mob drops start as raw materials. Use the menu on any row to make them from a Farmer's Delight recipe instead, or to turn a made item back into a raw one.
- **Which recipe.** When an item has several recipes, the planner prefers the main one over "from slices", "from crate" and salvaging recipes. Switch per row.
- **Tags.** An ingredient that accepts several items (a tag such as `#c:crops/onion`, or a list) shows one of them with "any of N". Pick a different item in the small menu; the shopping list follows.
- **Loops.** Recipes that would need the item you are making (crates and their crops, pies and their slices) are left out of that branch.
- **Quantities** are multiplied down the tree and rounded up to whole crafts. Cooking Pot meals add their serving container (usually a bowl) as an ingredient. Cutting Board tools lose durability but are not used up, so they are listed under stations.
