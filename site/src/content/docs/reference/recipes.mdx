All {meta.counts.recipes} recipes, drawn the way the game shows them: {meta.counts.cooking} for the [Cooking Pot](/mechanics/cooking-pot/), {meta.counts.cutting} for the [Cutting Board](/mechanics/cutting-board/), and the rest for the crafting table, furnaces, campfires and the smithing table.
Search for a result or an ingredient, or pick a station. Hover a slot for the item's name; slots that flip through items accept any of them.

To see what a dish takes all the way down to raw crops, use the [recipe planner](/reference/recipe-planner/). The {meta.counts.integrationRecipes} extra recipes for other mods are on [Compatibility](/compatibility/#recipes-for-other-mods).
