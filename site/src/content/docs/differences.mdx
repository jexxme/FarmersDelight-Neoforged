Farmer's Delight Neoforged is vectorwing's Farmer's Delight, brought to Minecraft 26.x on NeoForge. It has the content of Farmer's Delight {meta.modVersion.split('+')[0]}, so if you know the original, you already know this mod. This page covers the few places where it's different.

## What stays the same

The crops, meals, blocks and recipes are the same, with the same names. Switching over is safe:

- Remove the original Farmer's Delight and add this one. Your worlds keep their kitchens, farms and chests.
- Datapacks, add-on recipes and scripts made for Farmer's Delight keep working, because every item and recipe keeps its name.
- <Item id="canvas_sign" plural /> from older worlds keep their text, and old Baskets load as <Item id="bamboo_basket" plural />.
- Your old config file is still read, under the same name.

## What's new or fixed

- **REI support** on Minecraft 26.1.2 and newer, next to JEI. See [Compatibility](/compatibility/).
- **Fixes for problems the original still has**, from its [issue tracker](https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight/issues):
  - <Item id="rope_fence" plural /> and <Item id="rope_fence_gate" plural /> count as fences and fence gates, so recipes and mods that want "any fence" take them.
  - The <Item id="stove" /> can be lit with any fire starter, including Fire Charges and other mods' flint and steel.
  - Farmer villagers can compost <Item id="cabbage_seeds" /> and <Item id="tomato_seeds" />.
  - Shift-clicking meals out of the <Item id="cooking_pot" /> counts toward your crafting statistics.
  - Modpacks can make their own grasses drop <Item id="straw" /> by adding them to one tag.
  - Updated translations: Russian, Rusyn, Mexican Spanish, Indonesian, Turkish, Ukrainian and Brazilian Portuguese.

Everything else that changed, release by release, is in the [changelog](/changelog/).

## Known issues in 1.3.4

These all work again in the next update. Until then, here's what to expect in {issuesVersion} and how to work around it.

- **Placed pies can't be eaten by hand.** Use a knife on the pie to cut off slices, and eat those. Fixed in the next update.
- **<Item id="milk_bottle" />, <Item id="hot_cocoa" /> and <Item id="melon_juice" /> do nothing when you drink them.** Use a <Item id="minecraft:milk_bucket" /> or a Potion of Healing instead. Fixed in the next update.
- **The <Item id="rice_roll_medley_block" /> can't be served.** Make the rolls on their own; break a placed medley to get it back (it still has all {medleyFull} servings). Fixed in the next update.
- **<Item id="minecraft:rabbit_stew" /> gives no Nourishment**, though its tooltip lists {timeText(rabbitSoup.duration)}. Turning off **Rabbit Stew Buff** in the [config](/reference/config/#enableRabbitStewBuff) brings it back, with vanilla food values. Fixed in the next update.
- **<Item id="onion_soup" /> has the wrong stats:** {onion.nutrition} hunger, {sat(onion.saturation)} saturation and {timeText(nour('onion_soup'))} of Nourishment{sameAsPie ? ", the same as Shepherd's Pie" : ''}. Fixed in the next update.
- **{tierNames} give {timeText(nour(tier[0]))} of Nourishment instead of {timeText(medium)}.** Fixed in the next update.
- **A thrown <Item id="rotten_tomato" /> acts like a snowball**, without its splat. Fired from a Dispenser it works normally. Fixed in the next update.
- **Ropes don't react to an empty hand or a rope in hand:** sneaking to reel a rope back up, clicking with rope to extend it downward and ringing a bell hung on a rope don't work. Break ropes one by one, and sneak while placing rope. Fixed in the next update.
- **Bone meal used by hand on an unripe tomato vine does nothing.** Dispensers and Rich Soil still grow it. Fixed in the next update.
- **Village farms almost never grow Farmer's Delight crops**, even with the setting on. The original mod has the same bug. Fixed in the next update.
- **JEI has no info page for the <Item id="copper_knife" />.** REI has it. Fixed in the next update.
