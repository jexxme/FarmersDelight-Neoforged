export const wild = ['wild_cabbages', 'wild_onions', 'wild_tomatoes', 'wild_carrots', 'wild_potatoes', 'wild_beetroots', 'wild_rice'].map((k) => worldgen.find((w) => w.id === `farmersdelight:${k}`)).filter(Boolean);
export const bnames = (w) => w.biomes.slice(0, 3).map((b) => biomes[b]?.name ?? b.split(':')[1].replaceAll('_', ' '));
export const stew = items['farmersdelight:beef_stew'];
export const stewRecipe = recipeById.get('farmersdelight:cooking/beef_stew');
export const nour = stew.consumable.effects.find((e) => e.id === 'farmersdelight:nourishment');

<Render name="scenes/kitchen" float="right" size={230} caption="Where this guide ends up" />

This guide takes you from a fresh world to your first bowl of stew. You only need vanilla materials to start, and the
mod's crops come in along the way. Each step links to a page with more detail.

<Steps>

1. **Find wild crops.** Farmer's Delight scatters wild plants across the world. Break them for seeds and crops; each one
   grows in its own kind of place:

   <table>
   	<thead><tr><th>Plant</th><th>For example in</th></tr></thead>
   	<tbody>
   		{wild.map((w) => <tr><td><Item id={w.id} /></td><td>{bnames(w).join(', ')}{w.biomes.length > 3 ? ` and ${w.biomes.length - 3} more` : ''}</td></tr>)}
   	</tbody>
   </table>

   The full list with chances is on [Wild crops & worldgen](/mechanics/wild-crops/). Plant what you find on farmland, as
   described in [Crops](/mechanics/crops/).

2. **Make a knife.** A <Item id="flint_knife" /> needs only flint and a stick. Knives cut food on the Cutting Board, slice
   pies, and give extra drops from some animals; see [Knives](/mechanics/knives/).

   <div class="not-content"><Recipe id="flint_knife" /></div>

3. **Place a Cutting Board.** Put an item on it with a right-click, then right-click with the knife to cut it. Raw beef
   becomes minced beef, fish becomes slices. More on the [Cutting Board](/mechanics/cutting-board/) page.

   <div class="not-content" style="display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;gap:30px 40px"><Recipe id="cutting_board" /><Recipe id="cutting/beef" /></div>

4. **Build a Cooking Pot and something hot to put it on.** The <Item id="cooking_pot" /> needs heat from the block below:
   a campfire works from day one, a <Item id="stove" /> is the tidy kitchen version. The rules are on
   [Heat sources](/mechanics/heat-sources/).

   <div class="not-content" style="display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;gap:30px 40px"><Recipe id="cooking_pot" /><Recipe id="stove" /></div>

5. **Cook.** Place the pot on the lit campfire or Stove and right-click it to open it. Put the ingredients in the
   {pot.maxIngredients.value} slots in any order. Beef Stew needs only vanilla food: raw beef (or minced beef), a carrot
   and a potato. The arrow fills while the pot is hot; this recipe takes {timeText(stewRecipe.cookingtime)}.

   <div class="not-content"><Recipe id="cooking/beef_stew" /></div>

6. **Serve it.** The finished stew waits in the pot. Put a <Item id="minecraft:bowl" /> in the container slot, or simply
   right-click the pot holding a bowl, to get a bowl of stew. See [Cooking Pot](/mechanics/cooking-pot/#serving).

7. **Eat.** <Item id="beef_stew" /> fills <FoodStats nutrition={stew.food.nutrition} saturation={stew.food.saturation} compact />
   and gives <EffectBadge id={nour.id} name={nour.name} ticks={nour.duration} />: for {timeText(nour.duration)} your hunger
   bar doesn't go down. You get the bowl back. More on [Nourishment](/mechanics/effects/).

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Put a hopper between the heat source and the pot: it still passes the heat up, and it pulls finished meals out of the pot
for you. That is the first step to an automatic kitchen; see [Cooking Pot automation](/mechanics/cooking-pot/#automation).
</Aside>

## Where to go next

- Grill meat and fish {stove.slots.value} at a time on the [Stove](/mechanics/stove/), or fry them in seconds with a [Skillet](/mechanics/skillet/).
- Cook a [feast](/mechanics/feasts/) and share it out by the bowl.
