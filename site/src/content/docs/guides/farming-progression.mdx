Farmer's Delight farming grows in stages. You start with a handful of wild plants, turn them into fields, and end with soil that feeds your crops by itself. This guide walks the path in order; each step links to the page with all the details.

<Shot src="mechanics/rice-paddy.jpg" caption="A rice paddy: young rice in the shallow water up front, mature plants with golden panicles behind." />

## Find wild crops

<Render name="scenes/wild-crops" size={220} float="right" caption="Wild crops" />

Look for leafy tufts with small flowers:

- **Beaches:** <Item id="wild_cabbages" /> and <Item id="wild_beetroots" />, often with <Item id="sandy_shrub" plural />.
- **Temperate grass, forests and plains:** <Item id="wild_onions" /> and <Item id="wild_carrots" />.
- **Cold taigas and windswept hills:** <Item id="wild_potatoes" />.
- **Hot, dry biomes** (desert, savanna, badlands): <Item id="wild_tomatoes" />.
- **Swamps, jungles and beaches, standing in water:** <Item id="wild_rice" />.

They are rare: a wild onion patch spawns in about 1 in {wg('wild_onions').rarity} chunks, a beach cabbage patch in 1 in {wg('wild_cabbages').rarity}. When you find one, there are usually more nearby. Break them by hand for seeds, or with shears to take the plant itself.

The [Wild crops](/mechanics/wild-crops/) page has a biome picker and every patch's details.

<Aside type="tip" title="Short on seeds?">
Bone meal on a wild crop can plant a copy next to it. The wandering trader also sells seeds, and shipwrecks and village houses often carry some.
</Aside>

## Plant a first field

<Render name="scenes/garden" size={220} float="right" caption="A first garden" />

Till dirt near water and plant:

- <Item id="cabbage_seeds" /> and <Item id="onion" plural /> grow like wheat.
- <Item id="tomato_seeds" /> grow into a bush, then a vine you can pick again and again. Hang <Item id="rope" /> above it and it climbs.
- <Item id="rice" /> goes into shallow water, one block deep.

Growing times, harvesting and drops: [Crops](/mechanics/crops/).

## Get a knife

A <Item id="flint_knife" /> is cheap and opens up a lot: <Item id="straw" /> from grass and wheat, extra leather and ham from animals, and the [Cutting Board](/mechanics/cutting-board/). Straw becomes rope, canvas and bales ([Straw, rope & nets](/mechanics/straw-and-rope/)).

## Make Organic Compost

<Render name="scenes/compost-to-soil" size={220} float="right" caption="Compost and Rich Soil" />

<Item id="organic_compost" /> is crafted from dirt, straw, rotten flesh or tree bark and bone meal. Place it and leave it: it slowly rots into Rich Soil on its own.

Sunlight, water and other compost blocks next to it speed it up. One block alone under open sky takes about {checksToTime(N / sunny)}. A pile with water beside it goes much faster: about {checksToTime(N / best)} for the middle of a 3×3 layer next to water. Torches don't help; only sunlight counts.

<RecipeList output="organic_compost" />

The [Organic Compost](/mechanics/organic-compost/) page has a calculator for your setup.

## Farm on Rich Soil

<Item id="rich_soil" /> gives the plant on it a free bone meal now and then ({pct(boost, 0)} of the time it gets a turn). Till it into <Item id="rich_soil_farmland" /> for crops; it cannot be trampled. Saplings, berry bushes and rice planted on it get the boost too. See [Rich Soil](/mechanics/rich-soil/).

## Grow mushroom colonies

<Render name="scenes/colony-farm" size={220} float="right" caption="Mushroom colonies on Rich Soil" />

Put a brown or red mushroom on Rich Soil and it soon turns into a <Item id="brown_mushroom_colony" />. Colonies grow only on Rich Soil, from smallest to full size in about {checksToTime(colony.maxAge.value * colony.growth.value)}. Harvest a full colony with a knife for {colony.maxAge.value} mushrooms at once, and it grows back. See [Mushroom colonies](/mechanics/mushroom-colonies/).
