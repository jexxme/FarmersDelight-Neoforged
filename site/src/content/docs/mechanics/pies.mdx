## How it works

- **Placing.** Use a pie item on a block; it needs a rigid surface below <Cite f={pie.survive} />. The vanilla
  <Item id="minecraft:pumpkin_pie" /> places the mod's pumpkin pie block too; with `enablePumpkinPieSneakToPlace` (default
  **{String(sneak.default)}**) you have to sneak to place it, otherwise any use on a block places it <Cite f={pie.pumpkinPie} />.
- **Cutting a slice.** Right-click the pie holding a knife (any item with the knife ability, or in
  `#farmersdelight:tools/knives`) <Cite f={pie.isKnife} />. One slice item pops out toward you, you are not fed, and the pie
  shows one bite more. The last slice removes the block <Cite f={pie.cutSlice} />.
- **Eating from the block.** The pie has code to eat a slice straight from the block with an empty hand: it feeds you the
  slice's food value once and applies the slice's effect, without the item eating sounds <Cite f={pie.consumeBite} />. In this
  version the pie answers a right-click with no knife by passing, and Minecraft 26.3 only runs a block's empty-hand action
  when the block asks for it, so an empty-hand right-click does not reach that code <Cite f={pie.knife} /> <Cite f={pie.dispatch} />.
  Read from the code, not checked in game. Cut slices with a knife and eat those.
- A comparator reads the slices left <Cite f={pie.comparator} />.

Slices are quick to eat, and their Speed effect is applied without particles or an icon <Src path="common/FoodValues.java" lines="245-252" />. Slices also craft back into a whole pie (recipes below).

## Breaking a pie

The pie blocks have empty loot tables{emptyLoot ? '' : ' (not all)'}, so breaking one with anything but a knife drops
**nothing** <Src path="src/generated/resources/data/farmersdelight/loot_table/blocks/apple_pie.json" />. Broken with a knife,
a loot modifier drops one slice per slice left, {pie.pastry.value.pie} minus the bites taken <Cite f={pie.pastry} />.

<Aside type="caution">
Mine a placed pie with a knife, or cut it up first. A pickaxe or your fist destroys the whole pie.
</Aside>

## Cakes and candle cakes

With a knife in hand <Cite f={pie.knifeCake} />:

- **Cake.** Right-click: one <Item id={cakeSlice} /> drops and the cake loses a bite; after {pie.knifeCake.value} bites the
  next cut removes it, so a whole cake gives {pie.pastry.value.cake} slices. Breaking a cake with a knife drops
  {pie.pastry.value.cake} minus its bites in slices <Cite f={pie.pastry} />.
- **Candle cake** (any of the {candleCakes.length} in `#farmersdelight:drops_cake_slice`). Right-click: it turns into a cake
  with one bite taken, drops its candle, and drops one slice. Breaking a candle cake with a knife adds
  {candle.raw.count} slices to its normal drops <Src path="src/generated/resources/data/farmersdelight/loot_modifiers/slicing_candle_cake.json" />.
