Pies are food you place. Set one down and it becomes a block with {pie.bites.value} slices that you eat straight off
the plate or cut off with a [knife](/mechanics/knives/). The mod has {pies.length} pies, and it lets you slice the vanilla <Item id="minecraft:cake" />
the same way: a whole cake gives {pie.pastry.value.cake} slices.

## Cutting slices

Place the pie on a solid block. Then right-click it holding any knife: one slice pops out toward you and the pie shows
one bite more. The last slice removes the block. A vanilla <Item id="minecraft:pumpkin_pie" /> places as a pie block too
{sneak.default ? <>when you sneak and right-click a block with it</> : <>when you right-click a block with it; aim at the air to eat it instead</>}.

## Eating from the pie

Right-click a placed pie with an empty hand to eat one bite. A bite feeds you exactly like eating one slice, Speed
included, and the pie loses a slice; the last bite removes the block. You have to be hungry, just like eating anything
else. It's the quickest way to share a pie: put it on the table and everyone helps themselves.

## Slices

Each slice is a quick snack with a short burst of Speed. The Speed has no particles or icon, so don't be surprised when
you can't see it. Slices also craft back into a whole pie.

## Breaking a pie

Break a placed pie with a knife and you get one slice for every slice left on it. Break it with anything else, even your
fist, and **nothing{emptyLoot ? '' : ' (for most pies)'}** drops: the pie is gone. So always pick up a pie with a knife,
or cut it up first.

## Cakes and candle cakes

With a knife in hand:

- **Cake.** Right-click it and one <Item id={cakeSlice} /> drops while the cake loses a bite. Keep going and you get
  {pie.pastry.value.cake} slices from a whole cake. Breaking a cake with a knife also gives you every slice that's
  left on it.
- **Candle cake.** Right-click it and it drops its candle and one slice, and turns into a cake with one bite taken.
  Breaking a candle cake with a knife gives {candle.raw.count} slices on top of the candle.

## Good to know

- **Comparators** next to a pie read how many slices are left.
- **Support.** A pie needs a solid block under it and pops off otherwise.
