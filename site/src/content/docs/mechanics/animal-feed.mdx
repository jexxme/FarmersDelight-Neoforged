## Feeding your animals

**Dog Food** works on the tamed animals listed above. Each feeding uses one Dog Food and gives you the <Item id="minecraft:bowl" /> back.
It's also a normal bowl of food, so if you eat it yourself it restores {dog.food.nutrition / 2} hunger shanks.

**Horse Feed** works on any tamed horse-like mount. Horses also follow you while you hold it, just like they do for
{tempted.map((id, i) => <>{i ? (i === tempted.length - 1 ? ' and ' : ', ') : ''}<Item id={id} plural /></>)}. You can't eat it
yourself.

A heal plus buffs makes either one perfect right before a fight or a long ride. The items' tooltips also list what they
do.

## Recipes

Dog Food cooks in the [Cooking Pot](/mechanics/cooking-pot/), so it needs heat and a bowl to serve. Horse Feed is crafted.

## Good to know

- **Not for breeding.** Feeding heals and buffs the animal, but it doesn't breed it or count as its normal food.
- **Creative mode** doesn't use up the food.
