Two items are meant for your animals rather than for you. Feed them by right-clicking the animal with the item in hand.
Both heal the animal to full and give it a set of effects.

## How it works

**Dog Food** <Cite f={feed.dogUse} />: right-click a living animal from `#farmersdelight:dog_food_users` while holding Dog
Food. If the animal can be tamed it must already be tamed. Its health is set to the maximum, it gets the three effects, you
hear it eat and see a few stars. Outside creative you lose one Dog Food and get the <Item id="minecraft:bowl" /> back. Since
Dog Food is also a normal bowl food, you can eat it yourself when you are not aiming at a dog.

**Horse Feed** <Cite f={feed.horseUse} />: the same with `#farmersdelight:horse_feed_users`; tameable mounts must be tamed.
Full health, the two effects, a horse eating sound and stars, and one Horse Feed is used up (not in creative).
Horse Feed is also in `#minecraft:horse_tempt_items` ({tempted.map((id, i) => <>{i ? ', ' : ''}<Item id={id} /></>)}), so
horses follow you while you hold it.

Both items explain this in their tooltip ("When fed to…") while the client option `enableFoodEffectTooltip` is on
<Cite f={feed.dogTooltip} />.

## Details

- The tag `#farmersdelight:horse_feed_tempted` ({E['farmersdelight:horse_feed_tempted'].map((e, i) => <>{i ? ', ' : ''}{ent(e)}</>)})
  exists but is not read by any code; tempting uses the vanilla item tag above.
- Pack makers can add any living mob to the two "users" tags. Mobs that cannot be tamed accept the food without the tamed check.
- Feeding works on the mob directly and does not breed it or count as its normal food.
