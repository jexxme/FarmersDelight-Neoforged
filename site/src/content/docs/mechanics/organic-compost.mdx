## Setting up a heap

Every so often (on average every {checkSeconds()} seconds) each compost block gets a chance to rot one step further. After {N} steps it turns into Rich Soil. Its chance each time adds up from what's around it, in the {side}×{side}×{side} space centred on the block:

- **Activators: +{pct(compost.activator.value)} each.** Other compost, Rich Soil, mushrooms and mycelium all count, and the block counts itself: <Chips tag="farmersdelight:compost_activators" compact />
- **Sunlight: +{pct(compost.skyBright.value)}**, or only +{pct(compost.skyDim.value)} in the shade. Open sky above the heap or right next to it is enough.
- **Water: +{pct(compost.waterAdd.value)}** if there's any water in that space, even one waterlogged block.

<CompostCube />

So the best heap is a big pile of compost outdoors, with water running beside it. The block in the middle of a solid {side}-wide pile counts {side ** 3} activators: {pct((side ** 3) * compost.activator.value)} before sunlight and water.

<Aside type="tip" title="Torches don't help">
Only sunlight counts, and night counts as sunlight too. A torch-lit cellar always gets the shade bonus, while a heap under a glass roof still sees the sky.
</Aside>

## Good to know

- Breaking compost gives back {nameOf(drop)}, but its progress is lost: it starts again from step 1.
- Mushrooms survive on compost in any light, but [mushroom colonies](/mechanics/mushroom-colonies/) only grow on Rich Soil.
- Some villages come with a compost heap you can raid ([Villagers](/mechanics/villagers/)).
- A [canvas rug](/mechanics/straw-and-rope/#canvas-and-canvas-rug) on top lets sunlight through, so you can cover a heap and keep the bonus.
