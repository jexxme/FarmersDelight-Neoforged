<Item id="organic_compost" /> is a block that slowly rots into <Item id="rich_soil" />, the best soil in the mod. Place it, leave it, and come back later. Nothing needs to be added; the block works on its own and goes faster with sunlight, water and other "activator" blocks nearby.

<Shot src="mechanics/compost-heap.jpg" caption="An organic compost heap beside a pond, with mushrooms and rich soil around it to speed up composting." />

## How it works

The compost has a `composting` stage from 0 to {compost.stages.value} <Src {...cite(compost.stages)} />. On each random tick it adds up a chance, then rolls once <Src {...cite(compost.roll)} />:

| Source | Adds |
|---|---|
| Each block from `#farmersdelight:compost_activators` in the {2 * compost.cube.value + 1}×{2 * compost.cube.value + 1}×{2 * compost.cube.value + 1} cube around it, **itself included** | +{pct(compost.activator.value)} <Src {...cite(compost.activator)} /> |
| Highest sky light in the cube (read one block above each cell) is above {compost.skyThreshold.value} | +{pct(compost.skyBright.value)}, otherwise +{pct(compost.skyDim.value)} <Src {...cite(compost.skyThreshold)} /> |
| Any water in the cube (source, flowing, or a waterlogged block) | +{pct(compost.waterAdd.value)} once <Src {...cite(compost.water)} /> |

A success moves it one stage on; a success at stage {compost.stages.value} turns it into Rich Soil. That is **{N} successes** from fresh compost.

<Aside title="Torches don't help">
Only **sky** light counts, and the game's sky light value does not change between day and night <Vanilla {...cite(vanilla.skyBrightness)} />. A torch-lit cellar always gets the low light bonus. A compost pile outside under a roof of glass still sees the sky.
</Aside>

| Setup | Chance per random tick | Mean time to Rich Soil |
|---|---|---|
| {ex[0][0]} | {pct(ex[0][1])} | {randomTicksToTime(N / ex[0][1])} |
| {ex[1][0]} | {pct(ex[1][1])} | {randomTicksToTime(N / ex[1][1])} |
| {ex[2][0]} | {pct(ex[2][1])} | {randomTicksToTime(N / ex[2][1])} |
| {ex[3][0]} | {pct(ex[3][1])} | {randomTicksToTime(N / ex[3][1])} |

The math: one block gets a random tick on average every {vanilla.sectionTick.value} / {vanilla.randomTickSpeed.value} game ticks, because each 16×16×16 section picks {vanilla.randomTickSpeed.value} random positions per tick <Vanilla {...cite(vanilla.sectionTick)} />. The compost needs {N} successes at chance *p*, so on average {N} / *p* random ticks.

Big piles are faster than single blocks: the centre of a solid {2 * compost.cube.value + 1}-wide cube of compost counts {(2 * compost.cube.value + 1) ** 3} activators, {pct((2 * compost.cube.value + 1) ** 3 * compost.activator.value)} before light and water.

## Details

- The compost always random-ticks, also when nothing is around it <Src {...cite('common/block/OrganicCompostBlock.java:27-30')} />.
- Breaking it drops the block at any stage, but the stage is lost: it restarts at 0.

<BlockDrops block="organic_compost" />

- Mushroom colonies can sit on compost: it is in `#minecraft:overrides_mushroom_light_requirement`, so mushrooms survive on it in any light. They only grow on Rich Soil though ([Mushroom colonies](/mechanics/mushroom-colonies/)).
- Villages can generate compost heaps with Organic Compost ([Villagers](/mechanics/villagers/)).
- It gives off mycelium particles now and then <Src {...cite(compost.particles)} />.
