## Rope

Rope is a thin, climbable column with no collision box <Src {...cite(rope.noCollision)} />. It can float: placed rope needs no support <Src {...cite(rope.floats)} />, so you can hang it from a ceiling or run it in mid-air.

- **Placing on a block:** rope goes on the clicked face like any block. Placed against the side of a block it ties to sturdy faces, walls, iron bars and other rope next to it; placed from above or below it only links to rope, bars and walls <Src {...cite(rope.connect)} />.
- **Extending downward:** the rope item has code to extend a hanging column: used on a rope, it searches down the column to the first free block (water is fine, other fluids stop it) and places there; while sneaking it places on the clicked face instead <Src {...cite(rope.item)} />.
- **Bells:** a rope directly under a bell is marked `tied_to_bell` and drawn tied to it. The rope block also has code to ring a bell up to {rope.bell.value} ropes above when you right-click the column with an empty hand <Src {...cite(rope.bell)} />.
- **Reeling:** sneak-right-clicking a rope with an empty hand is coded to pull up the bottom rope of the column and give it to you (setting `enableRopeReeling`, default {String(cfg('enableRopeReeling'))}) <Src {...cite(rope.reel)} />.
- Only rope can replace rope; any other block placed into it fails <Src {...cite(rope.replace)} />.
- Rope is waterloggable, pathfindable, and [tomato vines](/mechanics/crops/#climbing-rope) climb it.

<Aside type="caution" title="From the code: bell ringing, reeling and downward extension in 26.3">
The rope block answers every click before the item or the empty hand gets a turn: with rope in hand it returns `SUCCESS`, with anything else `PASS` <Src {...cite(rope.useItemOn)} />. In vanilla 26.3 `SUCCESS` ends the click, and the empty-hand action only runs after `TRY_WITH_EMPTY_HAND` <Vanilla {...cite(vanilla.useDispatch)} />. Read that way, the downward extension (without sneaking), bell ringing and reeling do not run in this version, while sneak-placing does, because sneaking with an item skips the block. This is a code reading, not an in-game test.
</Aside>

## Rope fence and gate

<Item id="rope_fence" /> connects to other rope fences, rope fence gates and sturdy faces; vanilla fences do not connect to it <Src {...cite(rope.fence)} /> <Src {...cite(rope.fenceMixin)} />. Leads tie to it like a fence. The <Item id="rope_fence_gate" /> is solid when closed and has no collision when open <Src {...cite(rope.gate)} />.

## Safety net

<Item id="safety_net" /> is a thin net in the middle of the block (from {net.shape.value.from} to {net.shape.value.to} pixels up). Landing on it cancels all fall damage, and the net bounces you back up (bounce restitution {net.bounce.value}) <Src {...cite(net.fall)} /> <Src {...cite(net.bounce)} />. Sneak while landing to stop the bounce; you then take normal fall damage. Nets are waterloggable.

<RecipeList ids={['safety_net', 'rope_from_safety_net']} />

## Bales

- <Item id="straw_bale" /> behaves like a hay bale (it extends the vanilla hay block): falling on it takes {vanilla.hayFallFactor.value * 100}% of the damage <Src {...cite(bales.straw)} /> <Vanilla {...cite(vanilla.hayFallFactor)} />.
- <Item id="rice_bale" /> faces the side you click and also cuts fall damage to {bales.riceFall.value * 100}% <Src {...cite(bales.riceFall)} />.
- A campfire on top of either bale sends up tall signal smoke, like on hay <Src {...cite(bales.signal)} />.

<RecipeList ids={['straw_bale', 'rice_bale']} />

## Tatami

<Item id="tatami" /> is a woven block that pairs up. Place a tatami against the face of an unpaired tatami (without sneaking) and the two join into one long mat, turning toward each other <Src {...cite(tatami.pair)} />. Sneak to place it alone. Breaking one partner unpairs the other <Src {...cite(tatami.unpair)} />.

<Item id="full_tatami_mat" /> is a two-block floor mat placed like a bed; <Item id="half_tatami_mat" /> is one block. Both need a block below <Src {...cite(tatami.mat)} />.

## Canvas and canvas rug

<Item id="canvas" /> is made from straw and used for [canvas signs](/mechanics/storage-and-decoration/#canvas-signs), paintings, scaffolding and books. The <Item id="canvas_rug" /> is a 1-pixel floor covering that lets sky light through <Src {...cite(tatami.rug)} />.
