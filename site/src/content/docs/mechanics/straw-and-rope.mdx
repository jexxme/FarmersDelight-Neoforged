<Item id="straw" /> is the fibre of Farmer's Delight. You get it by cutting grass and crops with a [knife](/mechanics/knives/), and it becomes rope, canvas, bales and mats.

## Getting straw

Hold any knife and break grass, fully grown wheat, ripe rice panicles or sandy shrubs:

## Rope

<Item id="rope" /> is a thin column you can climb like a ladder, with nothing to bump into. It doesn't need support, so you can hang it from a ceiling or stretch it through mid-air.

- **Placing.** Put against the side of a block, rope ties onto it; it also links up with walls, iron bars and other rope next to it.
- **Hanging it down.** Right-click any rope in a column with more rope in your hand and the new piece goes on at the bottom, so you can lower a long rope from the top without climbing down. It extends through water, but not through lava.
- **Pulling it up.** Sneak and right-click a rope with an empty hand to reel in the bottom piece; it goes straight into your inventory. Server owners can turn this off in the [config](/reference/config/).
- **Bells.** Rope right under a bell gets tied to it. Right-click the column with an empty hand (without sneaking) to ring the bell at the top, up to {rope.bell.value} ropes above you. A bell pull for your village or base.
- **Tomatoes.** [Tomato vines](/mechanics/crops/#climbing-rope) climb rope and give you a tall tomato wall. They climb other mods' supports too, such as Vine & Barrel's trellis (NeoForge only): see [Trellises and other supports](/mechanics/crops/#trellises-and-other-supports).

### Rope fence and gate

<Item id="rope_fence" /> joins other rope fences, rope fence gates and solid blocks, but not wooden fences. Leads tie to it like to any fence. The <Item id="rope_fence_gate" /> blocks the way when closed and lets everything through when open.

## Safety nets and bales

<Item id="safety_net" /> is a thin net ({net.shape.value.to - net.shape.value.from} pixels) halfway up its block. Land on it and you take **no fall damage**, and it bounces you back up, keeping {Math.round(net.bounce.value * 100)}% of your falling speed. Sneak as you land to stop the bounce, but then you take normal fall damage. Nets can sit in water.

<RecipeList ids={['safety_net', 'rope_from_safety_net']} />

**Bales soften falls too.** A <Item id="straw_bale" /> works like a hay bale and a <Item id="rice_bale" /> does the same: falling on {vanilla.hayFallFactor.value === bales.riceFall.value ? `either does only ${Math.round(bales.riceFall.value * 100)}% of the damage` : `a straw bale does ${Math.round(vanilla.hayFallFactor.value * 100)}% of the damage, on a rice bale ${Math.round(bales.riceFall.value * 100)}%`}. A campfire on top of either one sends up tall signal smoke, like on hay.

<RecipeList ids={['straw_bale', 'rice_bale']} />

## Tatami and canvas

<Item id="tatami" /> is a woven block that pairs up. Place a tatami against an unpaired one and the two join into one long mat; sneak to place it on its own. Breaking one half unpairs the other.

<Item id="full_tatami_mat" /> is a two-block floor mat you place like a bed, and <Item id="half_tatami_mat" /> covers one block. Both need a block under them.

<RecipeList ids={['tatami', 'full_tatami_mat', 'half_tatami_mat', 'full_tatami_mat_from_halves', 'tatami_block_from_full']} />

<Item id="canvas" /> goes into [canvas signs](/mechanics/storage-and-decoration/#canvas-signs), paintings, scaffolding and books. A <Item id="canvas_rug" /> is a paper-thin floor covering that lets sunlight through, handy on top of an [Organic Compost](/mechanics/organic-compost/) heap.
