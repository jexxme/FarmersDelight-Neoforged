The <Item id="cutting_board" /> is where raw ingredients get cut up. Put an item on it, hit it with the right tool, and the
pieces fall off the side. Knives do the kitchen work (cuts of meat, sliced fish, dough and pie slices), while axes,
pickaxes, shovels, hoes and shears break down logs, stone, gravel and salvage. There are {cutting.length} recipes.

## How it works

1. **Place.** Right-click the board with an item in your **main hand**: the whole stack goes onto it, as much as fits
   <Cite f={board.use} />. Right-click again with an empty main hand to take it all back.
2. **Cut.** Right-click with a tool in your main hand. The board looks for a `farmersdelight:cutting` recipe that matches
   both the item on the board and the tool <Cite f={board.lookup} />. Each click:
   - uses {board.perCut.value} item from the board <Cite f={board.perCut} />;
   - rolls the results and throws them off the board's side;
   - costs the tool {board.toolWear.value} durability and counts as a use of it <Cite f={board.toolWear} />;
   - shows how many items are left on the board.
3. **No match.** With the wrong tool, or an item that has no recipe, you get "invalid tool" and nothing happens.

The board needs a solid top face or a centre support below it, and pops off otherwise <Cite f={board.survive} />.

## Tools

Recipes ask for a tool tag (or, for shears, the shearing ability). In this version:

| Tool | Tag | Items |
|---|---|---|
| Knife | `#c:tools/knife` | {knives.map((id, i) => <>{i ? ' ' : ''}<Item id={id} label="" /></>)} the {knives.length} knives |
| Axe | `#minecraft:axes` | any axe |
| Pickaxe | `#minecraft:pickaxes` | any pickaxe |
| Shovel | `#minecraft:shovels` | any shovel |
| Hoe | `#minecraft:hoes` | any hoe |
| Shears | shearing ability or `#c:tools/shear` | <Item id="minecraft:shears" /> and modded shears |

## Chance results and Fortune

Some results are not guaranteed. A result with a `chance` and a `count` rolls **each unit** separately, and keeps it when
a random number is at most `chance + bonus × Fortune level` <Cite f={board.chance} />. `bonus` is the config option
`cuttingBoardFortuneBonus`, **{fortune.default}** by default (range {fortune.range[0]}–{fortune.range[1]}; 0 turns it off).
Fortune is read from the tool doing the cutting, so a Fortune knife or axe helps.

## Carving display

Sneak and right-click the board with a **tool** (anything with a tool component), a trident or shears, and that single item
is laid on the board as decoration <Cite f={board.carveEvent} /> <Cite f={board.carveRule} />. While a tool is displayed the
board takes no other items and cannot cut <Cite f={board.carveBlocks} />; take it off with an empty hand. Items in
`#farmersdelight:flat_on_cutting_board` ({flat.map((id, i) => <>{i ? ', ' : ''}<Item id={id} /></>)}) lie flat instead of
standing up <Cite f={board.flat} />.

## Automation

- **Hoppers and pipes** see the board's single slot from every side, for inserting and extracting
  <Cite f={board.capability} />. A hopper can also pull a displayed tool off.
- **Dispensers.** With `enableCuttingBoardDispenserBehavior` on (default **{String(dispenser.default)}**), a dispenser
  facing a Cutting Board uses the item it would dispense as the cutting tool on whatever is on the board. The tool stays in
  the dispenser and only loses durability <Cite f={board.dispenserMixin} /> <Cite f={board.dispenser} />. While a board is in
  front, the dispenser never shoots its item out normally, even when the board is empty or the item is no tool.
- **Comparator.** Signal = <code>floor(count / max stack × {board.comparator.value}) + 1</code> while anything is on the board, 0 when
  empty <Cite f={board.comparator} />.

A dispenser of knives, a hopper feeding the board from above and a hopper under the drop point make a hands-free meat or
fish slicer.

## Sounds

Each cut plays, in order of priority <Cite f={board.sounds} />: the recipe's own `sound` ({withSound} recipes set one,
mostly axe stripping); the sheep-shearing sound for shears; the board's knife sound for knives; the break sound of the
block being cut, when the item is a block; otherwise wood breaking.

## Details

- Breaking the board drops the stack on it and the board <Cite f={board.breakDrop} />.
- The Cutting Board burns in a furnace for {fuel} ticks.
- A recipe may have at most {board.maxResults.value} different results <Cite f={board.maxResults} />.
- Each cut triggers the "use a cutting board" advancement criterion.
