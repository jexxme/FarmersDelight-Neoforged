The <Item id="cutting_board" /> is where raw ingredients get cut up. Put an item on it, hit it with the right tool, and the
pieces fall off the side. Knives do the kitchen work: cuts of meat, fish slices, dough and pie slices. Axes, pickaxes,
shovels, hoes and shears break down logs, stone, gravel and old gear. There are {cutting.length} recipes in all.

<Shot src="mechanics/cutting-board.jpg" caption="Prep counter: a cabbage waiting on one cutting board, an iron knife on the next, spruce cabinets below." />

## How to cut

1. **Put it on.** Right-click the board with the item in your main hand. The whole stack goes on, and you can top it up
   with more of the same item. Right-click with an empty hand to take it all back.
2. **Cut.** Right-click with the right tool in your main hand. Each cut uses {board.perCut.value} item from the board,
   drops the results to the side, and costs the tool {board.toolWear.value} durability.
3. **Wrong tool?** You get an "invalid tool" message and nothing happens.

Any knife works for food: {knives.map((id) => <Item id={id} label="" />)}. For blocks, any axe, pickaxe, shovel or hoe does
the job, and shears (modded ones too) salvage leather goods.

## Lucky drops and Fortune

Some drops only come sometimes; the percentage is shown in the corner of the slot. When a recipe gives two or more of a
lucky drop, each one rolls on its own. A **Fortune** tool adds {pct(fortune.default)} to every one of these chances per
level: the {pct(exChance)} {ex.outs[0].name} from {ex.input} becomes {pct(Math.min(1, exChance + 3 * fortune.default))}
with Fortune III. Knives and axes can both take Fortune.

<FortuneCalc client:visible rows={chanceRows} bonusDefault={fortune.default} range={fortune.range} />

The bonus per level can be changed in the [config](/reference/config/#cuttingBoardFortuneBonus); 0 turns it off.

## Automation

- **Dispenser slicer.** A dispenser facing a Cutting Board uses whatever it holds as the cutting tool on the item on the
  board. The tool stays in the dispenser and only wears down. Put a hopper above the board to feed it and one below where
  the pieces land, and you have a hands-free meat or fish slicer.
- **Watch out:** while a board is in front of it, a dispenser never shoots its item out, even when the board is empty.
  {dispenser.default ? 'You can switch this off in the config.' : 'This is off by default; switch it on in the config.'}
- **Hoppers** can put items on the board and take them off from any side.
- **Comparator.** A comparator reads how full the stack on the board is, from 1 up to {board.comparator.value + 1}.

## Good to know

- **Show off a tool.** Sneak and right-click the board with a tool, a trident or shears to lay it on the board as
  decoration. {flat.map((id, i) => <>{i ? (i === flat.length - 1 ? ' and ' : ', ') : ''}<Item id={id} /></>)} lie flat;
  everything else stands up. While a tool is on display the board can't cut; take it off with an empty hand.
- **Mind the empty board.** Right-clicking an empty board with a knife puts the knife on it like any other item.
- **Support.** The board needs a solid block under it, or something that holds up its middle like a fence, and pops off otherwise.
- **Breaking it** drops the board and the stack on it.
- **Fuel.** A Cutting Board burns in a furnace for {seconds(fuel)}.
