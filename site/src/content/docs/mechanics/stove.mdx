The <Item id="stove" /> is a brick cooker with two jobs. Its top is a grill that cooks {stove.slots.value} items at once,
anything a campfire can cook. And the whole block is a [heat source](/mechanics/heat-sources/) for a
<Item id="cooking_pot" /> or <Item id="skillet" /> set on top. It is placed already burning and gives light level
{B.maxLight} while lit.

<Shot src="mechanics/stove-grilling.jpg" caption="A lit stove grilling six raw cuts at once: beef, porkchop, chicken, mutton, salmon and cod." />

## Grilling

Right-click the Stove with raw food to lay one item on the grill. Do it again to fill the next spot, up to
{stove.slots.value}. Each item has its own timer, and when it is done the cooked food pops up off the grill for you to
pick up. Food that can't be grilled isn't placed; you just use it as normal.

<StoveGrill />

You can't take raw food back off by hand. Let it finish, break the Stove, or cover its top (below) to get it back. The
grill gives no experience.

## Lighting and putting it out

| To | Use | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| light it | <Item id="minecraft:flint_and_steel" /> | 1 durability |
| light it | <Item id="minecraft:fire_charge" /> | 1 fire charge |
| put it out | <Item id="minecraft:iron_shovel" label="any shovel" /> | 1 durability |
| put it out | <Item id="minecraft:water_bucket" /> | the bucket is emptied |

While the Stove is out, food stays on the grill but stops cooking. It loses progress
{timesAsFast(stove.cool.value, stove.cook.value)} as it gained it, down to raw, and picks up again once you relight:

## Keep the top clear

Anything in the block above that reaches down to the grill covers it, and a covered grill drops all its food and takes no
new food. That includes a Cooking Pot or Skillet on the Stove, so a Stove either grills **or** heats a pot, never both at
once. Carpets and bottom slabs cover it too; thin blocks that stay clear of the middle of the grill don't.

## Good to know

- **Hot feet.** A lit Stove burns anything standing on it for {hearts(stove.burn.value)} at a time. Sneak to walk across
  safely.{damageTags.includes('minecraft:is_fire') ? ' Fire Resistance protects you too.' : ''}{damageTags.includes('minecraft:no_knockback') ? ' The burn has no knockback.' : ''}
- **Mobs avoid it.** Mobs walk around a lit Stove as if it were fire.
- **Breaking it** drops the Stove and whatever is on the grill.
- **No hoppers.** Hoppers can't load or unload the grill. For automatic cooking, put a Cooking Pot on the Stove instead.
- **Any water bucket works,** modded ones included. In creative mode, lighting and putting out cost nothing.
