The <Item id="stove" /> is a brick cooker with two jobs. Its top is a grill that cooks {stove.slots.value} items at once
with campfire recipes, and the whole block is a [heat source](/mechanics/heat-sources/) for a
<Item id="cooking_pot" /> or <Item id="skillet" /> placed on it. It is placed already burning.

## How it works

<StoveGrill />

1. **Put food on.** Right-click the Stove holding an item that has a vanilla `campfire_cooking` recipe. One item goes onto
   the first free grill slot <Cite f={stove.placeFood} /> <Cite f={stove.placeSlot} />. This works lit or unlit. Items
   without a campfire recipe do nothing; the click passes on to the item (so food in hand is not eaten by accident when it
   can be grilled).
2. **Cooking.** Each slot has its own timer set to the recipe's `cookingtime`. While the Stove is lit, every slot gains
   {stove.cook.value} per tick; when a slot is done, the cooked item pops up out of the grill and the slot is free again
   <Cite f={stove.cook} />. The grill pays no experience.
3. **Cooling.** While it is unlit, every slot loses {stove.cool.value} progress per tick instead, down to zero
   <Cite f={stove.cool} />. The food stays on the grill.
4. **Taking food back.** Raw food cannot be taken off by hand. Break the Stove, cover its top, or wait for it to cook.

<CookDecay total={campfire[0].cookingtime} step={stove.cook.value} decay={stove.cool.value} label="One grill slot" />

## Lighting and putting it out

| While | Use | What it costs | |
|---|---|---|---|
| unlit | <Item id="minecraft:flint_and_steel" /> or any fire-starting tool | 1 durability (or the item, if it has none) | <Cite f={stove.ignite} /> |
| unlit | fire charge | 1 item, not in creative | <Cite f={stove.ignite} /> |
| lit | <Item id="minecraft:iron_shovel" label="any shovel" /> (tools that can douse) | 1 durability | <Cite f={stove.extinguish} /> |
| lit | <Item id="minecraft:water_bucket" /> (any `#c:buckets/water`) | the bucket is emptied, not in creative | <Cite f={stove.extinguish} /> |

A new Stove is always placed lit <Cite f={stove.placedLit} />. It gives light level {B.maxLight} only while lit (data:
block `maxLight`).

## Burns

A lit Stove hurts any living thing standing on its grill for {stove.burn.value.toFixed(1)} damage, as often as the game
applies step damage. **Sneaking** walks on it safely <Cite f={stove.burn} />. The damage type
`farmersdelight:stove_burn` is in {damageTags.map((t, i) => <>{i ? ', ' : ''}<code>#{t}</code></>)}
<Src path="src/generated/resources/data/minecraft/tags/damage_type/is_fire.json" />, so Fire Resistance blocks it and it
causes no knockback. Mobs path around a lit Stove as if it were fire <Cite f={stove.pathFire} />.

## Keep the top clear

The grill area is the box from {stove.grillArea.value.x[0]} to {stove.grillArea.value.x[1]} pixels across and
{stove.grillArea.value.y[1]} pixel tall, just above the Stove <Cite f={stove.grillArea} />. If the block above has any shape
inside that box, the top counts as **covered** <Cite f={stove.covered} />:

- all food on the grill drops as items on the next tick <Cite f={stove.coveredDrop} />;
- no new food can be put on.

A Cooking Pot or Skillet on the Stove covers it, so a Stove can grill *or* heat a pot, not both. Carpets, slabs and other
full-width blocks also cover it; thin blocks that stay out of the middle do not.

## Recipes

The grill uses vanilla `campfire_cooking` recipes, so everything a campfire cooks works, plus the
{campfire.length} the mod adds. The same recipes run in a placed or hand-held [Skillet](/mechanics/skillet/), much faster.

## Details

- Breaking the Stove drops whatever is on the grill, plus the Stove itself <Cite f={stove.breakDrop} />.
- Hoppers cannot load or unload the grill: the Stove registers no item handler (only the Cooking Pot, Cutting Board,
  baskets and cabinets do).
- Each occupied slot puffs smoke with a {stove.smoke.value * 100}% chance per tick <Cite f={stove.smoke} />.
- Hardness {B.hardness}, blast resistance {B.blastResistance}.
