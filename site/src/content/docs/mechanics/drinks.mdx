## How drinking works

- **The container comes back.** After drinking, the container is put in your inventory (or dropped if it is full), even
  from a stack of 16; the last drink of a stack turns into the container in your hand <Cite f={drink.container} />.
- **Effect removal** (`farmersdelight:remove_random_effects`) picks one of your active effects at random, skipping effects
  in an exclusion tag, and optionally only harmful ones <Cite f={drink.removeRandom} />. The exclusion tags
  `#farmersdelight:milk_bottle_ignored` and `#farmersdelight:hot_cocoa_ignored` ship empty, so packs can protect effects
  from them.
- **Healing** (`farmersdelight:heal`) heals the drinker directly <Cite f={drink.heal} />.
- Drinks with a food component (Apple Cider, Glow Berry Custard) can be drunk even with a full hunger bar, because their food
  is marked always edible.

<Aside type="note" title="Milk Bottle, Hot Cocoa and Melon Juice in this version">
{noFood.map((d, i) => <>{i ? ', ' : ''}<Item id={d.id} /></>)} have no food component. Farmer's Delight's drink item runs the
game's normal consume step, which is where these effects are applied, only for items that have food; without food it just
uses up the drink and hands back the bottle <Cite f={drink.foodPath} /> <Cite f={drink.vanillaFinish} />. Read from the code,
drinking these three therefore removes no effect and heals nothing in this version. Not checked in game.
</Aside>
