Farmer's Delight adds kitchen furniture that also stores things: cabinets in every wood, baskets that catch items, and crates and bags for crops. For decoration there are canvas signs, tatami and rugs.

<Shot src="mechanics/cabinets-baskets.jpg" caption="A kitchen storage corner with spruce cabinets, crates of carrots, potatoes, cabbages and onions, a rice bag and baskets." />

## Cabinets

A cabinet is a {storage.cabinetSlots.value}-slot container with a chest-style menu <Src {...cite(storage.cabinetSlots)} /> <Src {...cite(storage.cabinetMenu)} />. It opens toward you, shows its doors open while someone is looking inside, and plays open and close sounds. There is one per wood:

<Chips ids={cabinets} />

- Hoppers and pipes reach it from every side <Src {...cite('common/block/entity/CabinetBlockEntity.java:71-78')} />, and a comparator reads it like a chest <Src {...cite('common/block/CabinetBlock.java:82-85')} />.
- Opening one angers nearby piglins, like a chest <Src {...cite(storage.cabinetPiglins)} />.
- Broken, it drops its contents and itself; a custom name is kept.
- Wooden cabinets burn for {seconds(woodenFuel)}; {noFuel.map((c) => items[c].name).join(' and ')} do not burn.

<RecipeList ids={['oak_cabinet', 'bamboo_cabinet', 'crimson_cabinet']} />

The other woods follow the same pattern with their own slabs and trapdoors.

## Baskets

<Item id="wooden_basket" /> and <Item id="bamboo_basket" /> hold {storage.basketSlots.value} slots <Src {...cite(storage.basketSlots)} /> and **pick up item entities** in front of their opening. The opening faces you when you place it, so a basket placed while looking down opens upward <Src {...cite(storage.basketFacing)} />.

<BasketDiagram />

- **Pickup area:** the basket's own block plus one block in the direction it faces <Src {...cite(storage.basketArea)} />.
- **Speed:** it checks every tick; after a successful pickup it waits {storage.basketCooldown.value} ticks ({seconds(storage.basketCooldown.value)}) <Src {...cite(storage.basketCooldown)} />. Each pickup takes one item entity, as much of the stack as fits <Src {...cite(storage.basketCollect)} />.
- **Full:** when all slots are full stacks it stops collecting <Src {...cite(storage.basketFull)} />.
- **Redstone turns it off.** A powered basket does not collect <Src {...cite(storage.basketRedstone)} />. It starts enabled: the `enabled` state is not set on placement and a boolean property's first value is `true` <Src {...cite(storage.basketDefault)} /> <Vanilla {...cite(vanilla.booleanDefault)} />.
- **It never outputs.** A basket only collects; put a hopper under it to move items on. Hoppers can insert and extract on every side. Inserting into an empty basket also starts the {storage.basketInsert.value}-tick wait <Src {...cite(storage.basketInsert)} />.
- Baskets are waterloggable, and a comparator reads them like a chest.

<RecipeList ids={['wooden_basket', 'bamboo_basket']} />

<Aside type="tip" title="Auto-collecting farm">
Put a basket facing up under a block where drops land, for example under a slab path where crops are broken, or face it sideways into a water stream's end. Add a hopper below it to pass items into a chest.
</Aside>

## Crates and bags

Crates store nine crops as one block, and craft back into nine. <Item id="rice_bag" />, <Item id="rice_bale" /> and <Item id="straw_bale" /> do the same for rice, rice panicles and straw.

<RecipeList ids={[...crateRecipes, 'rice_bag', 'rice_bale']} />

The crates for vanilla crops ({gated.map((g) => items[g]?.name).filter(Boolean).join(', ') || 'carrot, potato and beetroot crates'}) are only craftable while `enableVanillaCropCrates` is {String(cfg('enableVanillaCropCrates').default)} (the default) <Src {...cite(storage.cratesCondition)} />.

## Canvas signs

<Item id="canvas_sign" /> and <Item id="hanging_canvas_sign" /> are signs with a canvas face. Craft one with any dye to colour the background; there are {tags.resolved.item['farmersdelight:canvas_signs'].length} standing and {tags.resolved.item['farmersdelight:hanging_canvas_signs'].length} hanging variants.

<CanvasSigns />

Signs with a dark background start with **white** text <Src {...cite(storage.signText)} />. Which colours count as dark is the setting `canvasSignDarkBackgroundList` (default {cfg('canvasSignDarkBackgroundList').default.join(', ')}) <Src {...cite(storage.signDark)} />.

<RecipeList ids={['canvas_sign', 'hanging_canvas_sign', 'red_canvas_sign']} />

## Tatami and rugs

<Item id="tatami" />, the tatami mats and the <Item id="canvas_rug" /> are made from straw and canvas. How tatami pairs up and how mats are placed is on [Straw, rope & nets](/mechanics/straw-and-rope/#tatami).

<RecipeList ids={['tatami', 'full_tatami_mat', 'canvas_rug']} />

## Details

- Baskets and cabinets keep a custom name when broken and support loot tables, so datapacks can fill them.
- Knives mine the tatami blocks, rugs and the rice bag fast (`#farmersdelight:mineable/knife`).
