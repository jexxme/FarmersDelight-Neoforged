Farmer's Delight adds kitchen furniture that also stores things: cabinets in every wood, baskets that catch items, and crates and bags for your harvest. For decoration there are canvas signs, and the straw blocks on [Straw, rope & nets](/mechanics/straw-and-rope/#tatami-and-canvas).

<Shot src="mechanics/cabinets-baskets.jpg" caption="A kitchen storage corner with spruce cabinets, crates of carrots, potatoes, cabbages and onions, a rice bag and baskets." />

## Cabinets

A cabinet holds {storage.cabinetSlots.value} stacks, like a single chest. It opens toward you and shows its doors open while someone is looking inside. There is one for every wood:

<Chips ids={cabinets} compact />

Hoppers can fill and empty it from any side, and a comparator reads it like a chest. Breaking it drops what's inside, and a renamed cabinet keeps its name.

<RecipeList ids={['oak_cabinet', 'bamboo_cabinet', 'crimson_cabinet']} />

The other woods follow the same recipe with their own slabs and trapdoors.

## Baskets

<Item id="wooden_basket" /> and <Item id="bamboo_basket" /> hold {storage.basketSlots.value} stacks and **pick up items lying in front of their opening**. The opening faces you when you place it, so a basket placed while you look down opens upward.

<BasketDiagram />

- **What it catches:** items in its own block and in the one block its opening faces.
- **How fast:** it grabs one dropped stack (as much as fits), then waits {seconds(storage.basketCooldown.value)} before the next.
- **Off switch:** a redstone signal stops it collecting. A new basket starts switched on.
- **It never pushes items out.** Put a hopper under it to send items on into a chest.
- When it's full, it stops collecting. A comparator reads it like a chest.

<RecipeList ids={['wooden_basket', 'bamboo_basket']} />

<Aside type="tip" title="Auto-collecting farm">
Face a basket up under a spot where drops land, or face it sideways at the end of a water stream that carries your harvest. A hopper below it passes everything on into a chest.
</Aside>

## Crates and bags

A crate packs nine crops into one block and crafts back into nine. The <Item id="rice_bag" />, <Item id="rice_bale" /> and <Item id="straw_bale" /> do the same for rice, rice panicles and straw.

<RecipeList ids={[...crateRecipes, 'rice_bag', 'rice_bale']} />

The {gated.slice(0, -1).join(', ')}{gated.length > 1 ? ' and ' : ''}{gated[gated.length - 1]} can be switched off in the [config](/reference/config/), but they're craftable by default.

## Canvas signs

<Item id="canvas_sign" /> and <Item id="hanging_canvas_sign" /> are signs with a canvas face. Craft one with any dye to colour it; there are {tags.resolved.item['farmersdelight:canvas_signs'].length} versions of each, plain one included.

<CanvasSigns />

On a dark background ({dark.join(', ')}) the text starts out **white** so you can read it.

<RecipeList ids={['canvas_sign', 'hanging_canvas_sign', 'red_canvas_sign']} />

## Good to know

- Opening a cabinet angers nearby piglins, just like a chest.
- Wooden cabinets burn in a furnace for {seconds(woodenFuel)}; {noFuel.map((c) => items[c].name).join(' and ')} don't burn.
- Baskets can sit in water.
- Knives break the rice bag quickly.
