A <Item id="brown_mushroom_colony" /> or <Item id="red_mushroom_colony" /> is a cluster of mushrooms that keeps growing back after you harvest it, like a tomato vine. Colonies only grow on [Rich Soil](/mechanics/rich-soil/), so they're the last step of the compost chain: compost becomes Rich Soil, Rich Soil turns a mushroom into a colony, and the colony gives you mushrooms forever.

<Shot src="mechanics/mushroom-colonies.jpg" caption="Brown and red mushroom colonies at different sizes on a bed of Rich Soil." />

## Starting a mushroom farm

<Steps>

1. Make some [Rich Soil](/mechanics/rich-soil/) from [Organic Compost](/mechanics/organic-compost/).

2. Place a brown or red mushroom on it. The next time the soil gets a turn, the mushroom becomes a small colony. This happens in any light, even in full daylight.

3. Wait for it to grow, then harvest with shears or a knife. The colony stays and grows back.

</Steps>

You can also find colonies on mycelium in mushroom fields (a patch in about 1 in {wg.rarity} chunks, see [Wild crops](/mechanics/wild-crops/#wg-brown_mushroom_colony)). They don't grow there, so take them home: break a full-size one with shears to get the colony itself, and put it on Rich Soil.

## Growing

A colony has {max + 1} sizes. Every so often it gets a {pct(growth, 0)} chance to grow one size, so from the smallest to full size takes about {checksToTime(max / growth)} on average.

<Stages block="brown_mushroom_colony" title="Brown mushroom colony" labels={stageLabels(max + 1, 'full size')} />

**Only on Rich Soil.** A colony on mycelium, podzol or anything else keeps its size forever. Rich Soil Farmland doesn't work either; it has to be plain Rich Soil.

**Bone meal** always works while the colony is below full size and adds {colony.bonemeal.value.min} or {colony.bonemeal.value.max} sizes. It works on any floor, so you can push a wild colony to full size before you take it.

## Harvesting

Right-click a colony that has grown at least once:

- **Shears** take **1** mushroom and shrink the colony by one size. Good for a steady trickle.
- **A [knife](/mechanics/knives/)** takes **every mushroom it has grown** at once, {max} from a full colony, and puts it back to the smallest size.

Either way the tool loses 1 durability and the colony stays. **Breaking** a colony gives more at once ({breakDrops.join(', ')} mushrooms from smallest to full size), but then it's gone.

## Where colonies survive

A colony stays put on these blocks in any light: <Chips tag="minecraft:overrides_mushroom_light_requirement" compact />

On any other solid block it needs the light to stay below {colony.survive.value}, like a plain mushroom.

## Good to know

- The red colony works exactly like the brown one, with red mushrooms.
- Colonies speed up nearby [Organic Compost](/mechanics/organic-compost/), like other mushrooms.
- Rich Soil gives colonies no free bone meal; their own growth is all they get.
- In a composter a colony always adds a layer ({pct(items['farmersdelight:brown_mushroom_colony'].compost, 0)} chance).
- Pick-block (middle click) on a colony gives you the plain mushroom.
