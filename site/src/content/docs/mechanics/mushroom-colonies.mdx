A <Item id="brown_mushroom_colony" /> or <Item id="red_mushroom_colony" /> is a cluster of mushrooms that grows bigger over time and can be harvested again and again, like a tomato vine. Colonies only grow on [Rich Soil](/mechanics/rich-soil/), which makes them the end point of the compost chain: compost becomes Rich Soil, Rich Soil turns a mushroom into a colony, and the colony keeps giving mushrooms.

<Shot src="mechanics/mushroom-colonies.jpg" caption="Brown and red mushroom colonies at different growth stages on a bed of rich soil." />

## Getting one

- **Plant a mushroom on Rich Soil.** A plain brown or red mushroom on Rich Soil becomes a colony of age 0 on the soil's next random tick <Src {...cite('common/block/RichSoilBlock.java:77-88')} />.
- **Find one.** Colonies generate in mushroom fields on mycelium, 1 patch in {wg.rarity} chunks, at a random age ([Wild crops](/mechanics/wild-crops/#wg-brown_mushroom_colony)). Break them with shears at full age to take the block, or place your own on Rich Soil.

## How it works

A colony has an age from 0 to {max} <Src {...cite(colony.maxAge)} />. On each random tick, a colony standing on <Chips tag="farmersdelight:mushroom_colony_growable_on" compact /> grows one age with chance {pct(growth, 0)} <Src {...cite(colony.growth)} />. From age 0 to {max} that is {max / growth} random ticks on average, about {randomTicksToTime(max / growth)}.

<Aside type="caution" title="Wild colonies don't grow">
Growth checks only `#farmersdelight:mushroom_colony_growable_on`, which holds Rich Soil alone. A colony on mycelium, podzol or anything else stays at its age forever.
</Aside>

### Harvesting

Right-click a colony of age 1 or more:

| Tool | Effect | Durability |
|---|---|---|
| <Item id="shears" /> (or any tool that can `shears_harvest`, or `#c:tools/shear`) | Age −1, drops **1** mushroom | −1 <Src {...cite(colony.shears)} /> |
| Any [knife](/mechanics/knives/) | Drops **as many mushrooms as the age**, age back to 0 | −1 <Src {...cite(colony.knife)} /> |

A knife takes a full colony's {max} mushrooms in one click; shears take them one at a time. Either way the colony stays and grows back.

### Bone meal

Bone meal always works while the colony is below age {max}, adding {colony.bonemeal.value.min} or {colony.bonemeal.value.max} ages <Src {...cite(colony.bonemeal)} />. It works on any floor, so it can push a wild colony to full size too.

## Breaking a colony

Breaking gives more mushrooms than a harvest, but the colony is gone. At full age, shears keep the block.

## Survival

A colony stays put if the block below is in `#minecraft:overrides_mushroom_light_requirement` (any light), or if the light at the colony is below {colony.survive.value} and the floor is a full solid block <Src {...cite(colony.survive)} />:

## Details

- Colonies are compost activators and speed up nearby [Organic Compost](/mechanics/organic-compost/).
- They do not get Rich Soil's bone meal boost (they are in `#farmersdelight:unaffected_by_rich_soil`); their own growth is faster.
- In a composter a colony always adds a layer (chance {items['farmersdelight:brown_mushroom_colony'].compost}).
- Pick-block on a colony gives the plain mushroom <Src {...cite('common/block/MushroomColonyBlock.java:133-136')} />.
- "Knife" here means an item with the `knife_harvest` ability or in `#farmersdelight:tools/knives` <Src {...cite(colony.isKnife)} />.
