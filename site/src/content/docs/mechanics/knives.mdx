Knives are Farmer's Delight's kitchen tool and a quick, light weapon. You need one for most [Cutting Board](/mechanics/cutting-board/) recipes, and a knife in hand turns many kills and harvests into extra drops: ham from pigs, leather from cows, straw from grass, slices from cakes and pies.

## Backstabbing

- It applies when the attacker stands in the zone behind the target: the attacker's direction and the target's look direction have a dot product below {knife.backstabAngle.value} <Src {...cite(knife.backstabAngle)} />. A successful backstab plays the critical-hit sound <Src {...cite(knife.backstabEvent)} />.
- Works for any attacker holding a knife with the enchantment, mobs included. It multiplies the final incoming damage.

## Mining

Knives mine `#farmersdelight:mineable/knife` at their mining speed and drop it correctly; everything else is mined at hand speed.

- Farmer's Delight blocks: <Chips ids={fdMineable} compact />
- Vanilla blocks: {vanillaGroups.map(([l, n]) => `${n} ${l}`).join(', ')}, plus <Chips ids={vanillaOther} compact />

## Details

- "A knife" in code means an item with the `knife_harvest` ability or in `#farmersdelight:tools/knives` <Src {...cite(colony.isKnife)} />. Knives have the abilities `shears_carve`, `knife_dig` and `knife_harvest` <Src {...cite(knife.abilities)} />. Cutting Board recipes check `#c:tools/knife` instead.
- Loot modifiers check `#farmersdelight:tools/knives` (mobs, pumpkins, pies) or `#farmersdelight:straw_harvesters` (straw), which both hold the {knives.length} knives.
- Knives break decorated pots, and the golden knife is loved by piglins (`#minecraft:piglin_loved`).
- Knives turn up in chests: butcher houses, bastions, ruined portals, end cities ([Chest loot](/mechanics/loot/)).
