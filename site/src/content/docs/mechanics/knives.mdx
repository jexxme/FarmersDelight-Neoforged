Knives are the kitchen tool of Farmer's Delight and a quick, light weapon. You need one for most [Cutting Board](/mechanics/cutting-board/) recipes, and with a knife in hand many kills and harvests give extra drops: ham from pigs, leather from cows, straw from grass, slices from cakes and pies.

## Extra drops with a knife

Kill with a knife in your main hand, or break the block with one:

<LootModifiers filter="scavenging_" title="Animals, mobs and pumpkins" />

Knives also give [straw](/mechanics/straw-and-rope/#getting-straw) from grass and crops. Break a placed cake or pie with a knife and you get the slices that were left (a cake has {vanilla.cakeMaxBites.value + 1}, a Farmer's Delight pie {knife.pieSlices.value}). Without a knife, a pie drops nothing ([Pies & cakes](/mechanics/pies/)).

## Right-click uses

| On | Right-click with a knife |
|---|---|
| Cake | Cuts off one <Item id="cake_slice" />; the last slice takes the cake |
| Candle cake | Drops the candle and a cake slice, and leaves the cake with one bite gone |
| Pumpkin | Carves it like shears and drops {knife.pumpkin.value} <Item id="minecraft:pumpkin_seeds" /> |
| [Mushroom colony](/mechanics/mushroom-colonies/) | Harvests every mushroom at once |
| Placed pie | Gives you one slice instead of eating it |

Ripe rice panicles broken with a knife drop rice grain and straw instead of the panicle ([Crops](/mechanics/crops/#rice)).

## Backstabbing

**Backstabbing** is a knife enchantment: hit a mob from behind and you deal more damage. It counts when you stand within about {behind}° of straight behind it.

<BackstabDiagram />

At level I it multiplies your damage by ×{mult(1)}, at II by ×{mult(2)}, at III by ×{mult(3)}. A backstab plays the critical-hit sound. It works for mobs holding an enchanted knife too, so watch your back. You can get it from {getBs.slice(0, -1).join(', ')}{getBs.length > 1 ? ' or ' : ''}{getBs[getBs.length - 1]}.

On top of Backstabbing, knives take the enchantments of these groups: {enchTags.join(', ')}. Sweeping Edge doesn't go on a knife.
