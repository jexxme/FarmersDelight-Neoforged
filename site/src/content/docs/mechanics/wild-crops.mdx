Wild crops are where every Farmer's Delight farm starts. They look like leafy tufts with a small flower on top and break as easily as tall grass. Break one by hand for its seeds or crop, and a single find is enough to start a field.

Seven kinds grow in the world: <Item id="wild_cabbages" />, <Item id="wild_onions" />, <Item id="wild_tomatoes" />, <Item id="wild_carrots" />, <Item id="wild_potatoes" />, <Item id="wild_beetroots" /> and <Item id="wild_rice" />. Beach patches also bring <Item id="sandy_shrub" plural />.

## Where to find them

Wild crops grow in patches: a fitting chunk only rarely has one, but a patch holds many plants, so when you spot one, look around for more.

## The wild crops

Each card shows the biomes, the ground the plant needs, what grows around it, and what it drops. Breaking with shears gives you the plant itself, to move it into your garden.

### Cabbages

Beaches, on sand, mixed with sandy shrubs. Breaking one by hand sometimes gives a whole cabbage on top of the seeds.

<WorldgenPanel id="farmersdelight:wild_cabbages" drops="wild_cabbages" />

### Sea beet

Sea beet is the wild beetroot. It grows on the same beaches as cabbages, often on the same stretch of sand.

<WorldgenPanel id="farmersdelight:wild_beetroots" drops="wild_beetroots" />

### Onions

One of the most widespread: mild grassland, forests and swamps. Wild onions grow among alliums and drop a few alliums too.

<WorldgenPanel id="farmersdelight:wild_onions" drops="wild_onions" />

### Carrots

The same mild biomes as onions. Look for a patch of coarse dirt in the grass.

<WorldgenPanel id="farmersdelight:wild_carrots" drops="wild_carrots" />

### Potatoes

Cold taigas and windswept hills, among ferns.

<WorldgenPanel id="farmersdelight:wild_potatoes" drops="wild_potatoes" />

### Tomato shrub

The tomato shrub is the wild tomato: hot, dry biomes such as deserts, savannas and badlands, among dead bushes. They never grow in wet biomes like jungles, and they take sand as well as dirt.

<WorldgenPanel id="farmersdelight:wild_tomatoes" drops="wild_tomatoes" />

### Rice

Wild rice is two blocks tall and stands in shallow water in swamps, jungles and on beaches.

<WorldgenPanel id="farmersdelight:wild_rice" drops="wild_rice" dropPools={[0]} />

### Mushroom colonies

Brown and red [mushroom colonies](/mechanics/mushroom-colonies/) grow on mycelium in mushroom fields, at a random size. Out there they never grow any bigger: colonies only grow on Rich Soil. {sameColonies ? 'Red colonies generate exactly like brown ones.' : ''}

<WorldgenPanel id="farmersdelight:brown_mushroom_colony" />

## Spreading and replanting

**Bone meal makes more.** On a wild cabbage, onion, tomato, carrot, potato or beetroot it works {pct(wild.bonemealChance.value, 0)} of the time and plants one copy a few blocks away. It stops once there are {wild.spreadLimit.value} of the same plant within {wild.spreadArea.value.xz} blocks. On wild rice it works {pct(wild.riceBonemeal.value, 0)} of the time and drops a <Item id="wild_rice" /> item instead of spreading.

**Sandy shrubs** never generate alone; they only come with beach patches. They need sand below them. Bone meal always works on a shrub and puts a fresh ring of shrubs around it, and breaking one with a [knife](/mechanics/knives/) gives <Item id="straw" /> {pct(shrubStraw, 0)} of the time.

**Replanting.** Wild crops go back down on <Chips ids={ground} compact />, so you can collect them with shears and plant a decorative patch at home.

## Good to know

- You can't place a block straight into a wild crop the way you can with grass. Break it first.
- They burn, and fire runs through a patch quickly.
- Oceans and caves show up in some biome lists, but a patch there needs dry ground under open sky, so you will hardly ever find one.
- Rich Soil gives wild crops no free bone meal.
