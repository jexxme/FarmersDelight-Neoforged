**Nourishment** is the reward for a proper meal: while it lasts, your hunger bar doesn't go down.

## What it does

{rows.length} foods give Nourishment, for {timeText(tiers[0][1])} up to
{timeText(tiers[tiers.length - 1][1])}.

While you have Nourishment, your hunger bar doesn't go down, however much you run, jump or fight. If you're hurt, the
game still spends your saturation on healing you as usual. Once that's gone, your hunger stays where it is and you keep
healing until the effect runs out (as long as your hunger bar is full enough to heal at all, as in vanilla).

- **You can always eat.** While Nourished you can eat even with a full hunger bar, so you can top it up with another
  meal before it runs out.
- **Golden hunger bar.** A gilded overlay covers the hunger bar while you're Nourished. You can hide it in the client
  [config](/reference/config/#enableNourishmentHungerOverlay).
- **No natural regeneration?** If the natural regeneration game rule is off, Nourishment freezes your hunger even when
  you are hurt.
