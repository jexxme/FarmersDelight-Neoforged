export const N = effects.find((e) => e.id === 'farmersdelight:nourishment');

Farmer's Delight adds two effects. **Nourishment** is the reward for a proper meal: while it lasts, your hunger bar
doesn't go down. **Comfort** is retired: it still exists, but nothing gives it any more.

## Nourishment

<EffectBadge id={N.id} name={N.name} /> {rows.length} foods give it, for {timeText(tiers[0][1])} up to
{timeText(tiers[tiers.length - 1][1])}.

Running, jumping, fighting and healing all make you hungry. Nourishment wipes that out, so at full health your hunger
and saturation stay exactly where they are. When you get hurt, your saturation is spent on healing as usual. Once the
saturation is gone, your hunger bar still stays put and you keep healing for as long as the effect lasts.

<NourishTimeline />

- **You can always eat.** While Nourished you can eat even with a full hunger bar, so you can top it up with another
  meal before it runs out.
- **Higher levels do nothing extra,** and food gives it without particles.
- **Only players** are affected.
- **Golden hunger bar.** A gilded overlay covers the hunger bar while you're Nourished. You can hide it in the client
  [config](/reference/config/#enableNourishmentHungerOverlay).
- **No natural regeneration?** If the natural regeneration game rule is off, Nourishment freezes your hunger even when
  you are hurt.

## Comfort (retired)

<EffectBadge id={C.id} name={C.name} /> No food gives Comfort any more: the foods that used to give it give Nourishment
now. You can still apply it with a command. While you're hurt it heals {hearts(effect.comfortHeal.value)} every
{timeText(effect.comfortInterval.value)}, but not while you have Regeneration or any saturation left. A scrolling overlay
on the health bar shows when it is working.
