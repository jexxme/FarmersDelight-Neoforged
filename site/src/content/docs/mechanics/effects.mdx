Farmer's Delight registers two status effects. **Nourishment** is the reward for a proper meal: while it lasts, your
hunger bar does not go down. **Comfort** is retired: it still exists in the game, but nothing gives it.

## Nourishment

<EffectBadge id={N.id} name={N.name} /> <span class="fd-source">“{N.description}” (in-game description)</span>

### Exactly what it does

Every tick, for players only <Cite f={effect.nourishment} />:

1. If the `naturalHealthRegeneration` game rule is on **and** you are hurt **and** you have saturation left, it does nothing
   this tick. Your saturation can be spent on healing as usual <Cite f={effect.healingCheck} />.
2. Otherwise it sets your **exhaustion to zero**. Exhaustion is what running, jumping, fighting and healing build up to
   drain saturation and then hunger, so neither goes down.

So hunger and saturation are frozen while you are at full health. When you are hurt, saturation is used for healing as in
vanilla. Once saturation reaches zero, step 2 applies again: the hunger bar stays where it is, and natural healing keeps
going for as long as the effect lasts, because its cost is wiped every tick.

<NourishTimeline />

- **Always edible.** While Nourished you can eat even with a full hunger bar <Cite f={effect.alwaysEat} />, so you can top
  up a running Nourishment with another meal.
- **Amplifier** is ignored. Foods give it as level I, with no particles and no icon in the inventory list
  <Cite f={effect.hidden} />.
- **Mobs** are not affected; it only works on players.
- **HUD.** A gilded overlay covers the hunger bar while Nourished, controlled by the client option
  `enableNourishmentHungerOverlay` (default {String(overlay.default)}) <Cite f={effect.hud} />.

### Vanilla soups and Rabbit Stew

With `enableVanillaSoupExtraEffects` on (default **{String(soupCfg.default)}**), eating a vanilla soup also grants
Nourishment <Cite f={effect.soups} /> <Cite f={effect.soupEvent} />:

<table>
	<thead><tr><th>Food</th><th>Nourishment</th><th>Food value</th><th>With the defaults</th></tr></thead>
	<tbody>
		{soupEffects.map((s) => {
			const v = vf(s.item);
			const rabbit = s.item === 'minecraft:rabbit_stew';
			return (
				<tr>
					<td><Item id={s.item} /></td>
					<td><span class="fd-val">{duration(s.duration)}</span> <small>{tierOf(s.duration).toLowerCase()}</small></td>
					<td>{v ? <FoodStats nutrition={v.nutrition} saturation={v.saturation} compact /> : '—'}</td>
					<td>{rabbit ? <>no Nourishment: see below</> : 'Nourishment applied'}</td>
				</tr>
			);
		})}
	</tbody>
</table>

**Rabbit Stew.** With `enableRabbitStewBuff` on (default **{String(rabbitCfg.default)}**), Rabbit Stew gets stronger food
values (shown above, read from the running game) <Cite f={effect.rabbitBuff} />. The same switch makes the soup handler skip
Rabbit Stew entirely, so with both defaults on it grants **no** Nourishment <Cite f={effect.rabbitSkip} />. Its tooltip still
lists the Nourishment line <Cite f={effect.rabbitTooltip} />. Turn the buff off to get the {duration(soupEffects.find((s) => s.item === 'minecraft:rabbit_stew').duration)} Nourishment
back at vanilla food values.

The vanilla soups also stack to 16 while `enableStackableSoupItems` is on (default
**{String(cfg('enableStackableSoupItems').default)}**).

## Comfort (retired)

<EffectBadge id={C.id} name={C.name} /> <span class="fd-source">“{C.description}” (in-game description)</span>

Comfort is still registered, so commands and other mods can apply it, but **no food or item in this version grants it**:
the changelog for 1.3.0 says the foods that used to give it give Nourishment instead
<Src path="changelog.md" lines="138-140" />. The item data agrees: no food lists Comfort. If you apply it yourself, every {effect.comfortInterval.value}
ticks ({duration(effect.comfortInterval.value)}) it heals {effect.comfortHeal.value} health while you are hurt, but skips the
heal if you have Regeneration or, for players, any saturation left <Cite f={effect.comfort} />. A scrolling overlay on the
health bar shows when it is working (client option `enableComfortHealthOverlay`, default {String(comfortOverlay.default)})
<Cite f={effect.comfortHud} />.

<Aside type="note">
Some older descriptions of the mod still say that warm soups give Comfort. In this version they give Nourishment.
</Aside>
