Farmer's Delight adds its own loot to {tables.length} kinds of vanilla chest: seeds and rope in mineshafts and shipwrecks, knives in bastions and end cities, onions in pillager outposts, and crops in village houses. It also adds drops to some mobs and blocks.

## How it works

Each chest addition is a global loot modifier of type `farmersdelight:add_loot_table`: when the game fills a vanilla chest with a matching loot table, it rolls the Farmer's Delight table too and adds the result <Src path="src/main/java/vectorwing/farmersdelight/common/loot/modifier/FDAddTableLootModifier.java" lines="38-43" />. The vanilla loot stays as it was.

Turn it off with <code>{opt.key}</code> (default {String(opt.default)}) in <code>{opt.file}</code>: when false, nothing is added <Src path="src/main/java/vectorwing/farmersdelight/common/Configuration.java" lines="107-108" />. Chests that were already generated keep their contents.

How to read a table: each **pool** rolls the listed number of times, and each roll picks one entry by weight. The bar is the chance per roll. "Chance a chest has it" combines all rolls of all pools.

## Details

- The chest-loot option only affects the `add_loot_table` modifiers. The mob and block modifiers above are always active.
- Enchanted knives in bastions and ruined portals use `enchant_randomly` from `#minecraft:on_random_loot`, which includes Backstabbing; end city knives are enchanted as at a level range instead.
- Chest names follow the vanilla loot table ids; the tables themselves are in `data/farmersdelight/loot_table/chests/`.
