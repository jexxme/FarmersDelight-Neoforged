export const ages = (b) => blocks[`farmersdelight:${b}`].properties.age.length;
export const field = cropRoll(1 + 3 + 8 * (3 / 4)); // vanilla crop speed on moist farmland with moist farmland all around

export const vine = cropRoll(crops.tomatoSpeed.value);
export const rice = cropRoll(crops.riceChance.value);
export const panicle = cropRoll(1);
export const tomatoTime = (crops.budMaxAge.value + 1) / field + crops.tomatoMaxAge.value / vine;

export const panicleMeal = bonemealSplit(crops.paniclesBonemeal.value);

Farmer's Delight adds four crops. <Item id="cabbage" /> and <Item id="onion" /> grow just like wheat. <Item id="tomato" label="Tomatoes" /> start as a bush, turn into a vine you can pick again and again, and climb rope. <Item id="rice" /> grows in shallow water and pushes up panicles on top.

You can't buy these crops from farmers, so your first seeds come from [wild crops](/mechanics/wild-crops/), chests or the wandering trader (see [getting seeds](#getting-seeds)).

## Planting and growing

Each crop gets a chance to grow one stage every so often: on average every {checkSeconds()} seconds. In a normal field on moist farmland that chance is {oneIn(field)}. Crops need light to grow, and the times below are averages without bone meal or [Rich Soil](/mechanics/rich-soil/).

| Crop | Plant it on | Light | Ready in about |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Item id="cabbage_seeds" /> | farmland | {vanilla.cropRandomTick.value}+ | {checksToTime((ages('cabbages') - 1) / field)} |
| <Item id="onion" /> | farmland | {vanilla.cropRandomTick.value}+ | {checksToTime((ages('onions') - 1) / field)} |
| <Item id="tomato_seeds" /> | farmland | {crops.budTick.value}+ | {checksToTime(tomatoTime)} to the first tomatoes |
| <Item id="rice" /> | water, one block deep | {crops.riceTick.value}+ above the water | {checksToTime(ages('rice') / rice)} to the first panicles |
| <Item id="rice_panicle" label="Rice panicles" /> | grow on top of rice | {vanilla.cropRandomTick.value}+ | {checksToTime((ages('rice_panicles') - 1) / panicle)} more |

**Plant in rows, not solid blocks.** Like wheat, a crop grows half as fast ({oneIn(dense)}) when the same crop sits next to it on two sides at a right angle, or on a diagonal. Single rows with a different crop or a water channel in between stay at full speed.

## Cabbages and onions

Plant <Item id="cabbage_seeds" /> or an <Item id="onion" /> on farmland and wait. Both have {ages('cabbages')} stages. A ripe cabbage gives a head and seeds, often several seeds. An onion is its own seed, and even an unripe onion gives the onion back, so breaking one early costs you nothing.

## Tomatoes

<Item id="tomato_seeds" /> grow into a small bush first. After {crops.budMaxAge.value + 1} stages the bush turns into a tomato vine, which needs {crops.tomatoMaxAge.value} more stages to fruit.

**Pick ripe tomatoes with an empty hand.** Right-click a ripe vine to get {crops.tomatoHarvest.value.min} or {crops.tomatoHarvest.value.max} tomatoes, with a {pct(crops.tomatoHarvest.value.rotten, 0)} chance of an extra <Item id="rotten_tomato" />. The vine drops back to its first vine stage and grows new fruit, so you never replant. Breaking a ripe vine gives the tomatoes and a seed back, and the vine is gone.

### Climbing rope

Hang <Item id="rope" /> above a vine and it climbs. Every so often the top of the vine grows onto the rope above, ripe or not, until it covers {crops.tomatoClimb.value} rope blocks. Each rope block becomes its own vine that ripens and gets picked on its own, so one seed can give you {crops.tomatoClimb.value + 1} vines' worth of tomatoes.

<TomatoClimb />

Vines on rope need light {crops.tomatoSurvive.value}+ or a view of the sky to stay alive. Breaking one leaves the rope in place, ready for the vine below to climb again.

## Rice

Plant <Item id="rice" /> in a **full water source block, one block deep**, standing on <Chips tag="minecraft:substrate_overworld" compact />. It won't go into flowing water, and clicking dry dirt or farmland with rice shows a hint instead. Rice checks the light **just above the water** ({crops.riceTick.value}+), not at the plant.

After {ages('rice')} stages, mature rice grows <Item id="rice_panicle" label="Rice Panicles" /> on top if the space above is free. The panicles have {ages('rice_panicles')} stages of their own and need light {crops.paniclesSurvive.value}+ or open sky.

**Harvest the panicles, not the rice.** Ripe panicles drop a <Item id="rice_panicle" />, or rice grain plus <Item id="straw" /> if you break them with a [knife](/mechanics/knives/). Unripe panicles drop nothing. Breaking the plant in the water gives back one grain and takes the panicles with it.

**Speed up the panicles.** They grow slowly on their own ({oneIn(panicle)} each time, about {checksToTime((ages('rice_panicles') - 1) / panicle)} from first to ripe). Put [Rich Soil](/mechanics/rich-soil/) under the rice: its free bone meal goes to the rice, and mature rice passes it on to the panicles.

## Bone meal

| Plant | One bone meal does |
|---|---|
| Cabbage, onion | +{vanilla.cropBonemeal.value.min} to {vanilla.cropBonemeal.value.max} stages, like wheat |
| Tomato bush | Always works: +{crops.budBonemeal.value.min} to {crops.budBonemeal.value.max} stages; growth past the bush carries over into the vine |
| Tomato vine | +{vineMeal.map((x) => x.stages).join(' or ')} stages, with a {pct(crops.tomatoBonemealClimb.value, 0)} chance to also climb the rope above. On a ripe vine it climbs, or feeds the vine above |
| Rice | Always works: +{crops.riceBonemeal.value.min} to {crops.riceBonemeal.value.max} stages; extra growth goes into the panicles. Not once the panicles are ripe |
| Rice panicles | +1 stage, but {pct(panicleMiss, 0)} of the time nothing happens |

<Aside type="caution" title="Known issue in 1.3.4">Bone meal used by hand on a growing tomato vine does nothing (and isn't used up). Use a dispenser with bone meal, or let [Rich Soil](/mechanics/rich-soil/)'s free bone meal do it.</Aside>

## Getting seeds

- **Wild crops.** Break <Item id="wild_cabbages" />, <Item id="wild_tomatoes" />, <Item id="wild_onions" /> or <Item id="wild_rice" /> for seeds, or cut them on a [Cutting Board](/mechanics/cutting-board/). [Wild crops](/mechanics/wild-crops/) shows where they grow.
- **Chests.** Shipwrecks, mineshafts, dungeons and village houses carry seeds and crops ([Loot](/mechanics/loot/)).
- **Wandering trader.** Sells {wandering.map((t) => nameOf(t.gives.id)).join(', ')} for {wandering[0].wants.count} emerald each ([Villagers](/mechanics/villagers/)).
- **Your own crops.** Tomatoes and rotten tomatoes craft into seeds, and rice panicles give rice grain.

## Good to know

- A tomato vine keeps trying to climb after it ripens, so you can add rope at any time.
- [Rich Soil Farmland](/mechanics/rich-soil/) can't be trampled and accepts every crop that grows on farmland, other mods' crops included.
- Rotten tomatoes only come from ripe vines, whether you pick or break them.
- Farmer villagers plant {planted.map((i) => nameOf(i)).join(', ')}{planted.includes('farmersdelight:rice') ? '' : ', but not rice'} ([Villagers](/mechanics/villagers/)).
