The <Item id="skillet" /> is a frying pan. Cook with it **in your hand** near a fire, or **place it** on a heat source as
a small, fast grill. It cooks everything a campfire or the [Stove](/mechanics/stove/) can, in a fraction of the time: food
that takes {timeText(t0)} on a campfire is done in {timeText(skilletTicks(t0, 0))}. It also hits hard, for
{hearts(dmg)}.

<Shot src="mechanics/skillet-campfire.jpg" caption="A skillet set on a campfire, searing a steak outdoors." />

## Cooking in your hand

1. Hold the Skillet in one hand and raw food in the other, and stand close to heat: any heat source in the
   {reach}×{reach}×{reach} blocks around you counts, even an unlit campfire or Stove. Being on fire works too.
2. Hold right-click. One item goes into the pan and starts frying.
3. Keep holding until it's done. The cooked food goes into your inventory.

Let go early and you get the raw item back. You can't start frying underwater, and food that can't be grilled shows a
hint instead.

## Cooking on a placed Skillet

Sneak and right-click a block with the Skillet to place it; a normal right-click doesn't. Set it on a lit heat source,
just like a [Cooking Pot](/mechanics/heat-sources/).

- **Right-click with food** to put the whole stack in. The Skillet must be empty first: it won't take more of a stack
  that is already cooking.
- **Each item** fries in the Skillet time, then gets tossed out the side, and the next one starts.
- **Right-click with an empty hand** to take the stack back.
- **No heat:** the current item loses progress {timesAsFast(skillet.tick.value)} as it cooked. A waterlogged Skillet
  drops its food and won't take more.

## Cook time and Fire Aspect

The Skillet takes {pct(skillet.factor.value)} of the campfire time, rounded down to whole seconds. Each level of **Fire
Aspect** on the Skillet takes off another {pct(skillet.perFireAspect.value)} of the campfire time, but nothing cooks
faster than {timeText(skillet.minTime.value)}. A placed Skillet keeps the Fire Aspect it had when you placed it.

## As a weapon

The Skillet hits for **{dmg} damage** ({hearts(dmg)}) with extra knockback, but it is slow: attack speed {spd}. A fully
charged hit lands with a heavy clang. It has {I.durability} durability, loses 1 per hit and 1 per block you mine with it,
and is repaired with {repair.length ? repair.map((id, i) => <>{i ? ', ' : ''}<Item id={id} /></>) : 'iron'}.

It takes {enchants.map((e, i) => <>{i ? (i === enchants.length - 1 ? ' and ' : ', ') : ''}{e}</>)}, but never Sweeping
Edge. Fire Aspect does double duty: it sets mobs alight and makes your cooking faster.

## Good to know

- **Breaking a placed Skillet** drops its food and the Skillet itself, with its enchantments, durability and name intact.
- **Held vs placed heat.** In your hand an unlit campfire or Stove counts as heat; a placed Skillet needs it lit.
- **No hoppers.** Nothing can load a placed Skillet automatically.
- **Sparks.** A placed Skillet with Fire Aspect gives off little sparks, more with higher levels.

<Aside type="tip">
For the fastest hand cooking, stand next to a Stove: it counts as heat even when it's out, and it only burns you if you
stand on top of it.
</Aside>
