The <Item id="skillet" /> is a frying pan you can cook with in two ways: **held in your hand** near a fire, or **placed**
on a heat source like a small, fast grill. It cooks the same `campfire_cooking` recipes as a campfire or the
[Stove](/mechanics/stove/), in a fraction of the time, and it doubles as a heavy weapon.

## Two ways to cook

| | Held in hand | Placed as a block |
|---|---|---|
| Set up | Skillet in one hand, food in the other | Sneak and use the Skillet on a block <Cite f={skillet.sneakPlace} /> |
| Heat | You are on fire, or a block in `#farmersdelight:heat_sources` is within the 3×3×3 cube around you. Lit state is **not** checked <Cite f={heat.heldNear} /> | A lit heat source directly below, or two below through a hopper ([heat rules](/mechanics/heat-sources/)) |
| Batch | one item per use | one stack, cooked one item at a time |
| Time | the Skillet formula below | the same formula, per item |
| Result | goes to your inventory (or drops) | thrown out to the side of the Skillet |
| Fire Aspect | read from the held Skillet | read from the Skillet that was placed <Cite f={skillet.storedSkillet} /> |

### Cooking in hand

1. Hold the Skillet in one hand and a campfire-cookable food in the other, near heat.
2. Hold use. One item is taken from the stack and starts frying <Cite f={skillet.heldUse} />. Without heat nearby the Skillet does
   nothing and the click passes to your other hand; with heat but food that has no campfire recipe, you get a hint message.
3. Keep holding until the cook bar fills: the use time is the Skillet cook time <Cite f={skillet.heldDuration} />.
   The cooked item goes into your inventory <Cite f={skillet.heldFinish} />.
4. Let go early and the raw item comes back <Cite f={skillet.heldRelease} />. Underwater the Skillet refuses to start.

### Cooking on a placed Skillet

- **Sneak-place** it; a normal right-click with the Skillet does not place it <Cite f={skillet.sneakPlace} />.
- **Right-click with food** while the Skillet is **empty**: the whole stack (as much as fits) goes in. A partly filled
  Skillet does not accept more, and food without a campfire recipe shows "invalid item" <Cite f={skillet.addItem} />.
- **Right-click with an empty hand** to take the stack back <Cite f={skillet.placedUse} />.
- While heated it adds 1 progress per tick; each finished item is thrown out to the Skillet's side, one input is used and the
  timer restarts <Cite f={skillet.output} />. Without heat the progress falls by {skillet.tick.value} per tick
  <Cite f={skillet.tick} />.
- A waterlogged Skillet drops its food and refuses new food <Cite f={skillet.tick} />.

## Cook time and Fire Aspect

Both ways use one formula <Cite f={skillet.formula} />:

1. Take the recipe's time in ticks ({skillet.defaultTicks.value} if it is 0) and turn it into whole seconds (÷ 20, rounded
   down).
2. Multiply by a factor of {skillet.factor.value}, minus {skillet.perFireAspect.value} per level of
   **Fire Aspect** on the Skillet.
3. Round down to whole seconds, back to ticks (× 20).
4. Keep it between {skillet.minTime.value} ticks ({seconds(skillet.minTime.value)}) and the recipe's own time.

## Details

- Breaking a placed Skillet drops the food and the Skillet item with all its components, so enchantments, durability and a
  custom name survive <Src path="src/generated/resources/data/farmersdelight/loot_table/blocks/skillet.json" />.
- A placed Skillet on fire, lava or a campfire shows a tray, like the Cooking Pot <Cite f={heat.traySkillet} />.
- The held check counts an **unlit** campfire or Stove as heat, while the placed Skillet needs it lit.
- No hopper or pipe can load a placed Skillet.
- Block hardness {blocks['farmersdelight:skillet'].hardness}.

<Aside type="tip">
For the fastest held cooking, stand next to a Stove: it counts as heat even when it is out, and it does not burn you
unless you stand on it.
</Aside>
