The <Item id="cooking_pot" /> is the heart of the kitchen. Put up to {pot.maxIngredients.value} ingredients in, set it on a
heat source, and it cooks them into a meal. Finished servings wait inside the pot until you scoop them out with the
right container, usually a <Item id="minecraft:bowl" />. It cooks {cooking.length} recipes in this version.

## How it works

<PotFlow />

1. **Ingredients.** The six slots are shapeless: any arrangement works, but the number of filled slots must equal the
   number of ingredients in the recipe, so a stray extra item blocks the match <Cite f={pot.shapeless} />.
2. **Heat.** The pot only cooks with a heat source directly below it, or two below with a <Item id="minecraft:hopper" />
   in between. See [heat sources](/mechanics/heat-sources/).
3. **Cook time.** Every tick that the pot is heated, holds a matching recipe and has room for the result, its progress
   goes up by {pot.cookStep.value} <Cite f={pot.cookStep} />. The target is the recipe's `cookingtime`
   ({pot.defaultCookTime.value} ticks when a recipe leaves it out). If the heat goes away, or the ingredients stop
   matching, progress does not reset: it falls by {pot.decay.value} per tick until it reaches zero <Cite f={pot.decay} />.
4. **Finishing.** One meal goes into the meal slot and every ingredient slot loses one item <Cite f={pot.finish} />.
   Ingredients that leave something behind (a bucket, a bowl, a bottle) pop out of the side of the pot as an item instead of
   staying inside <Cite f={pot.eject} />.
5. **Meal slot.** Servings pile up here, up to {pot.mealCap.value} even for meals that stack to 16, as long as each new
   meal is the same item with the same components <Cite f={pot.canCook} />. A different meal stops the pot until the slot is
   emptied.
6. **Container and output.** A meal that needs no container moves to the output slot by itself. Otherwise each matching
   container in the container slot turns one serving into a finished meal in the output slot <Cite f={pot.autoOutput} />.

### Which container a meal needs

The container is the recipe's `container` field if it has one, otherwise the meal's crafting remainder (the item you get
back after eating it) <Cite f={pot.containerRule} />. Shipped recipes use:

## Serving

<PotGui />

- **Right-click with the container.** Holding the meal's container (any stack size) and right-clicking the pot takes
  one serving straight from the meal slot: one container is used, the meal goes to your inventory or drops at your
  feet <Cite f={pot.serveClick} /> <Cite f={pot.serveTake} />.
- **Right-click with anything else** (or an empty hand) opens the pot's screen. Items are never put in by clicking the block.
- **Sneak with an empty hand** to switch the pot between hanging from its handle and standing <Cite f={pot.handleToggle} />.
- **Shift-click** in the screen sends `#farmersdelight:serving_containers` (<Item id="minecraft:bowl" />,
  <Item id="minecraft:glass_bottle" />, <Item id="minecraft:bucket" />) and the pot's current container to the container
  slot; everything else goes to the ingredient slots <Cite f={pot.shiftClick} />.
- **From the crafting grid.** A Cooking Pot item that holds a meal, crafted together with exactly one matching container,
  gives one meal and returns the pot with one serving fewer <Cite f={pot.foodServing} />.

## Automation

<HopperFaces />

The pot exposes two views of its inventory to hoppers and pipes <Cite f={pot.hopperSides} /> <Cite f={pot.hopperHandler} />:

| Face | Insert into | Extract from |
|---|---|---|
| Top | ingredient slots | ingredient slots |
| Sides and bottom | container slot | output slot |

The meal slot is never reachable. A hopper under the pot both pulls finished meals and, sitting on a heat source, passes
the heat up, so one column of *source, hopper, pot* is a complete automatic kitchen once a hopper on top feeds ingredients
and one on the side feeds bowls.

**Comparator.** The signal is the average fill of all nine slots: <code>floor(average × {pot.comparator.value}) + 1</code> when anything
is inside, 0 when empty <Cite f={pot.comparator} />.

## Experience

Each recipe has an `experience` value (shipped: {[...new Set(xps)].sort().join(', ')}; {pot.defaultXp.value} when left
out <Cite f={pot.defaultXp} />). The pot counts how many times each recipe finished <Cite f={pot.xpTrack} />, and pays out
`floor(count × experience)` orbs plus one more with the leftover fraction as the chance <Cite f={pot.xpSplit} />. It pays
when a player takes items from the output slot, or when the pot is broken <Cite f={pot.xpTake} /> <Cite f={pot.breakSide} />.
Serving by right-click and hoppers do not pay out; the experience stays stored in the pot.

## Breaking the pot

- The ingredient, container and output slots drop as items, and the stored experience pops out <Cite f={pot.breakDrops} />.
- The meal slot is **kept in the pot item**, together with its container and a custom name: the loot table copies
  `farmersdelight:meal`, `farmersdelight:container` and `minecraft:custom_name`, and only drops when the pot survives the
  explosion <Src path="src/generated/resources/data/farmersdelight/loot_table/blocks/cooking_pot.json" lines="5-20" />.
  Place it again and the servings are still there; pick-block in creative also copies them <Cite f={pot.pickBlock} />.
- The pot item stacks to {items['farmersdelight:cooking_pot'].stack}.

## Details

- **Placement.** The pot faces you. Placed against the underside of a block it hangs by its handle; otherwise it shows a
  tray when the block below is a tray heat source (fire, lava, campfires) <Cite f={pot.placement} />. The tray state updates
  when the block below changes, unless the pot is hanging <Cite f={pot.traySupport} />. It can be waterlogged.
- **Effects.** While heated it bubbles ({pot.bubbles.value.bubble * 100}% chance per tick) and steams
  ({pot.bubbles.value.steam * 100}%) <Cite f={pot.bubbles} />, and plays a boiling sound one time in
  {pot.boilSound.value}, a thicker one when a meal is stored <Cite f={pot.boilSound} />.
- **Block.** Hardness {potBlock.hardness}, blast resistance {potBlock.blastResistance}.
- **Mixing meals.** A pot holding servings of one meal cannot cook a different one until the meal slot is empty, even with
  free room <Cite f={pot.canCook} />.

<Aside type="tip">
Shift-clicking a stack of bowls into the screen lets the pot fill the output slot on its own, one meal per bowl, up to a
full stack.
</Aside>
