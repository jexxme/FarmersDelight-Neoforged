The <Item id="cooking_pot" /> is the heart of your kitchen. Put in up to {pot.maxIngredients.value} ingredients, set the
pot on something hot, and it cooks them into a meal. The servings wait inside until you scoop them out, usually with a
<Item id="minecraft:bowl" />. Most of the {cooking.length} pot recipes take {timeText(usual)}, and the pot holds up to
{pot.mealCap.value} servings at once.

## Cooking a meal

1. **Fill it.** Right-click the pot to open it and put the ingredients in, in any order. Use exactly what the recipe
   asks for: one extra item in a slot and nothing cooks.
2. **Heat it.** The pot needs something hot right below it, like a lit campfire or a <Item id="stove" />, or two blocks
   below with a <Item id="minecraft:hopper" /> in between. See [heat sources](/mechanics/heat-sources/).
3. **Wait.** The arrow fills while the pot is hot. Most meals take {timeText(usual)}; a few take
   {others.map((t) => +(t / 20).toFixed(2)).join(' or ')} seconds.
4. **Serve it.** Each finished meal uses one of every ingredient and lands in the meal slot. Scoop it out with its
   container (next section).

<PotFlow />

**If the heat goes out,** you don't lose the meal. The arrow drains back {timesAsFast(pot.decay.value, pot.cookStep.value)}
as it filled, and picks up again as soon as the heat is back. A short gap only costs a few seconds:

<CookDecay total={pot.defaultCookTime.value} step={pot.cookStep.value} decay={pot.decay.value} heatedFor={120} coldFor={40} label="A meal" />

## Serving

The easy way: hold the meal's container and right-click the pot. One serving goes straight into your inventory, no screen
needed. You can also put a stack of containers in the container slot and take finished meals out of the output slot.

<PotGui />

Each meal needs its own container, usually the one you get back after eating it. Meals that need none go straight to the
output slot.

The meal slot holds up to {pot.mealCap.value} servings, even of meals that only stack to 16. It holds one kind of meal at
a time: to cook something else, empty it first.

<Aside type="tip">
Shift-click a stack of containers into the pot's screen. {serving.map((id, i) => <>{i ? (i === serving.length - 1 ? ' and ' : ', ') : ''}{nameOf(id)}s</>)} (and whatever the current meal needs) jump to the container slot by themselves, and the pot then fills
the output slot one meal per container.

## Automation

One column of heat source, hopper and pot is a complete automatic kitchen. The hopper under the pot passes the heat up and
pulls finished meals out at the same time. Add a hopper on top to feed ingredients and one on the side to feed bowls.

<HopperFaces />

A comparator next to the pot reads how full it is: the more items in its {slotCount} slots, the stronger the signal, from 1
up to {pot.comparator.value + 1}.

## Good to know

- **Breaking the pot keeps the meal.** The servings in the meal slot stay inside the pot item, so you can pick up a pot of
  stew and set it down somewhere else. Everything in the other slots drops. An explosion can destroy the pot, servings
  and all.
- **Experience.** Each meal is worth {Math.min(...xps)} to {Math.max(...xps)} experience. You only collect it when you
  take meals out of the output slot by hand, or when you break the pot. Scooping with a right-click or a hopper leaves it
  stored in the pot until then.
- **Containers come back.** Ingredients that leave something behind in a crafting table, such as a bucket of milk, give
  it back here too, popped out of the side of the pot. The same goes for
  {giveBack.map(([to, from], k) => <>{k ? '; ' : ''}{from.map((id) => <Item id={id} label="" />)} → <Item id={to} /></>)}.
- **Serving from the item.** A pot item that holds a meal can be crafted with one matching container. You get one meal
  and the pot back with one serving fewer.
- **Hanging pot.** Place the pot against the underside of a block and it hangs by its handle. Sneak and right-click with
  an empty hand to switch between hanging and standing. On fire, lava or a campfire it stands on a small tray instead.
  Heat works the same either way.
- **Recipe book.** The pot's screen has a recipe book{book.default ? '' : ' when you switch it on'}. You can turn it
  {book.default ? 'off' : 'on'} in the [config](/reference/config/#enableCookingPotRecipeBook).
